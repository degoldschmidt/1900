# Project "1900"

A historically grounded spy life-sim / RPG set between 1895 and 1925, with deep systems of travel, deception, items, economy and politics; a world that moves on its own clock; a player who is hunted.

This repository currently holds the **design decision process**, run by a ten-member agentic game-design committee.

- `committee/CHARTER.md` — the brief (five base directives), the roster, rules of order, voting method
- `committee/DECISION_LOG.md` — chronological record of every decision and event
- `committee/round-1A-proposals/` — ≥10 raw proposals per panelist
- `committee/round-1B-synthesis/` — compiled pool, reviews, draft and final list of 20 candidates
- `committee/round-1C-voting/` — ballots, tally, finalist pitches
- `pitches/index.html` — the designed pitch document delivered to the owner (open it in a browser; `pitches/build.py` regenerates it from the committee's files)

## Outcome of decision round 1

Ten panelists filed 120 proposals; the panel nominated, merged and flagged them into 20 ratified candidates (30 amendments, all carried unanimously); a secret Borda ballot ranked them. Finalists, each written up as a full pitch and audited for history, engine feasibility and hook honesty:

1. **C07 — The Departure Is the Turn** (Borda 188/200): the railway timetable as the game's clock; every verb lives in the slack before a train that leaves without you.
2. **C01 — Several Masters, One Truth** (185): several intelligence services pay you at once, each holding its own model of you, reconciled only on the diplomatic calendar.
3. **C04 — The Legend Portfolio** (181): cover identities as maintained assets that keep running in other cities; the hunt advances only by linking two names.
4. **C17 — The Paper Hunter** (155): never observed, only recorded; hunters read only the institutional records that reached them.
5. **C13 — Faster Than the Wire** (145): your body moves at train speed, news of you at cable speed; race your own description and trade in the gap.

`1910.py` and `images/` are a 2015 prototype stub and are unrelated to the committee process.
