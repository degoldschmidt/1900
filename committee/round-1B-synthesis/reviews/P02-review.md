# Round 1B-1 Review — Simulation Engine Architect (Panelist P02)

## Overall reading of the pool
The pool is remarkably convergent: across ten lenses the same six structures recur — hunters that read only institutional records (P08.01, P04.05, P03.01, P08.05, P02.05), a two-speed world of wire against rail (P01.06, P02.02, P04.06), legends as owned entities with their own heat (P08.03, P09.03, P10.02, P04.12, P02.04), several paymasters holding separate beliefs (P07.01, P05.09, P09.11, P02.10), bounded history (P07.02, P02.03) and world-generated commissions (P01.02, P02.12, P09.07, P07.05). That convergence is good news for directive 5: it is one engine, not twelve, and the merges below are where the spine is shared. The strongest hooks are fairness made mechanical (the honest hunter and the trail autopsy), the departure as the unit of play, and loss aversion over things the player built (legends, households, networks). What is thin: conversation is systemic in only one panelist's work (P05.03, P05.02), coding appears mostly as cable copies rather than play, and almost nobody addresses how a dozen ledgers are presented without a spreadsheet — the engine must offer one consistent "ledger view" pattern. Only P03.11 offers a scoping path (a vertical slice), which the committee will need.

## Merge proposals (up to 5)
### M-P02-1 — One Clock: The Departure Is the Turn
- **Members:** P02.01, P01.01, P09.01, P04.02
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The world is a discrete-event simulation whose visible face is the timetable and the diary. Decision points are departures; between them the player fills city windows with errands, meetings and cables; waiting has costs (expenses, hunters, expiring offers); granularity stretches from minutes at a frontier to days at sea. The connection is the atomic tension unit: delays drawn from line and season, dead hours at junctions where cables catch up and money drains, a missed last train forcing a registered hotel night. Sessions end aboard trains; saves are keyed to world time and legs resolve deterministically on load. The same queue carries the other cadences the pool proposes: three clocks (P01.09), the obligation calendar (P09.06) and the annual audit (P10.11).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists converged on one structure; merged, it gives a single pacing system with the engine (P02.01), the verb structure (P01.01), the routing model (P04.02) and the retention logic (P09.01) instead of four overlapping candidates.

### M-P02-2 — Faster Than the Wire
- **Members:** P01.06, P02.02, P04.06, P09.05
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Two graphs: a transport graph on which bodies move by timetable, and a communications graph of offices with hours, jurisdictions and latencies on which facts move. Every fact about the player (name, description, photograph, warrant) is an entity propagating at its medium's speed; the player sees an estimated front and outruns it, slows it (a bribed clerk, a closed office, a missing liaison agreement, a cut cable) or changes what it describes by switching legend. The same layer delivers the world to the player as stale news (P09.05) and reprices bourses on arrival (P03.08, P02.09), so one propagation engine drives the hunt, the information economy and the market.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Three independent "race your own description" proposals plus the player-side mirror become one legible mechanic, and a single propagation code path for all facts is the largest scalability win in the pool.

### M-P02-3 — Bounded History: Tramlines, Ladders and Weather
- **Members:** P07.02, P02.03, P07.03, P07.04
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Macro-history is a tiered dependency graph: Tier 0 rails (war dates, revolutions, treaties), Tier 1 switchpoints that move within jittered windows (who wins a contract, which day a frontier closes), Tier 2 free variables. Crises run as explicit ladders of rungs with parameter deltas and permanent ratchets; wars are regime overlays on edges, prices, law and faction powers with internal phases; pressure accumulators with saturation guarantee no act moves a rail. Every state delta carries provenance, so at death, capture or retirement an inquest shows the player's diff against history, traced through the graph. City regime states (P07.11) and the prescience accumulator (P07.08) are rows in the same schema.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** It unites the accuracy contract (P07.02), the mechanisms of pressure (P07.03, P07.04) and the payoff of seeing one's fingerprints (P02.03) in one data schema that a new decade extends with rows, not code.

### M-P02-4 — Everything That Moves Has an Itinerary
- **Members:** P02.06, P04.11, P01.12, P06.12
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The player plans operations as dependency graphs of tasks with time windows and fallbacks (P02.06) and dispatches couriers, consignments, letters, cash and sub-agents as itinerary-holding entities on the same timetable graph (P04.11, P06.12), acting remotely by cable and post with latency, corruption and interception risk (P01.12). The engine validates plans against the timetable, shows slack, compiles them into scheduled events and re-plans affected sub-trees when the world intrudes; a compromised node cascades through everything in flight. Hunters and factions use the identical planner and may pursue a crate as readily as a person.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** One planner and one itinerary-holder entity type serve player, NPCs, mail, cargo and hunters, so the span-of-control and operations fantasies arrive at near-zero marginal engine cost while travel stays the primary verb.

### M-P02-5 — The World Posts Its Own Jobs
- **Members:** P01.02, P02.12, P09.07, P07.05
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** No authored missions. Factions derive goals from world state, and their plans emit typed wants (document, introduction, delivery, assessment) with windows, locations and value curves that rise toward the related crisis and collapse on supersession or publication. The player is one candidate among NPC agents; accepting one offer forecloses others by travel time alone; lapsed offers resolve and are reported (taken by a rival, cooled with the crisis); fulfilment perturbs the state that generates the next wants, and holding a prize is itself evidence.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists described the same generator from four lenses (loop, engine, retention, politics); merged, it is the content engine of directive 5 — volume from templates, consequence from dependency links, scarcity from the timetable — rather than four partial boards.

## Role-lens notes
Feasibility and scale audit. The pool converges on one engine spine, and it runs on an ordinary machine if four rules hold. (1) One scheduler: a global discrete-event queue with total ordering (time, priority, insertion id), so a save is seed plus input log and replay is exact; the autopsy (P08.09), inquest (P02.03), reveal calendar (P09.02) and annual audit (P10.11) are replays or batched evaluations of that queue. (2) One record: every act emits a typed, provenance-tagged record (place, time, identity tag, channel, fidelity, latency, persistence) into an append-only log; dossiers (P02.05, P09.10), trace ledgers (P08.01, P04.05, P03.01), item provenance (P06.03, P02.07), legend heat and linkage (P08.03), routine mining (P10.10) and archives (P08.12, P02.11, P03.02) must all be indexed views over that one log, never parallel stores, and archives hold ids, not copies. (3) Two graphs, one overlay layer: a date-versioned time-expanded transport graph (routing, reachability, alibi tests, cordons) and a communications graph with office hours, jurisdiction gating and latency; regimes (P04.04, P07.04, P03.05, P07.11, P01.10) are one parameter layer keyed to the macro calendar, not three. (4) Knowledge at institutions: facts live at institution nodes and in per-faction belief stores; NPCs query their institution; only a hot set holds individual beliefs (P05.03). Decay, heat, legend health and skill rust are evaluated lazily on access (P10.01), never per tick. Cost is then linear in player actions and bounded by hot sets; hierarchical reachability with bitsets per time slice makes P08.05's hypothesis propagation microseconds per hunter. Conflicts: P05.11's per-NPC episodic memory breaks rule 4; anything depending on generated prose or free text (P01.11 articles, P05.05 plaintexts, P05.12 notes) must be templated. Reconcile P08.05 with P09.02 by propagating reachability continuously but reweighting at institutional cadence. Build P03.11's Neutral Hub as the vertical slice.
