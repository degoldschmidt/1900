# Round 1B-1 Review — Pursuit & Hidden-Information Designer (Panelist P08)

## Overall reading of the pool
The pool converges far more than it diverges: at least eight proposals specify the same evidence model (hunters read only typed records with latency, fidelity and persistence — P01.04, P02.05, P03.01, P04.05, P08.01, P09.09, P08.02, P03.02), six bind the hunter to the same timetable and wire as the player (P01.06, P02.02, P02.08, P04.06, P08.05, P08.07), nine make the legend an entity with its own file (P01.03, P02.04, P03.10, P04.12, P05.01, P06.07, P08.03, P09.03, P10.02), and five describe the many-mastered agent whose patron turns hunter (P01.07, P02.10, P05.09, P07.01, P09.11). The strongest hooks are the departure as the turn (P01.01/P04.02/P09.01/P02.01), the dossier as a filtered view of the event log (P02.05), the race between the body and its own description (P01.06/P02.02/P04.06), legends you build and burn (P09.03 cluster) and the archive that never closes (P02.11/P08.12). Relative to the directives, conversation is nearly absent outside P05.03 and P05.08; physical surveillance, witnesses and face recognition are thin (P08.06, P03.10's laundry marks, P10.07's scars); the player-as-hunter inverse appears once (P08.11); and, most importantly for Directive 4, the pool has not settled what the player legitimately sees of the hunters' belief — the single question that decides whether forty hours of being hunted feels fair or arbitrary. The whole-game concepts are mostly three games wearing different titles (commission/concession agent, multi-identity life, race against the wire), which is good news for synthesis.

## Merge proposals (up to 5)
### M-P08-1 — The Paper Hunter (one evidence engine)
- **Members:** P02.05, P04.05, P08.01, P08.10
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every player act emits a typed record — legend used, place, time, channel or mode, fidelity, delivery latency, persistence, reader set — into one append-only log. A service's dossier is a filtered view of that log holding only the records that reached it through its own jurisdiction and liaison channels (latency parameters from P03.01's jurisdiction tables). Hunters deduce over their dossier, never over ground truth. The player sees an estimate of their own trail and may thin it (cleaning a register writes a record too) or forge it: records carry a hidden authorship field; a fabrication that the timetable makes impossible is discarded and raises that service's scepticism against the evidence class, so decoys are an arms race, not an exploit. Dossiers are items — stolen, read, a page removed, a false sighting inserted — and every edit is itself a logged event.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists independently specified the same data model; unified, one log serves the hunt, save and replay, item provenance and the autopsy, and false evidence becomes a first-class deception verb instead of a special case.

### M-P08-2 — Hunters on the Timetable (hunter reasoning and action)
- **Members:** P08.05, P02.08, P04.06, P08.07
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Hunters are agents who cannot teleport. Each holds a bounded set of weighted hypotheses about each legend (place, time, mode, habit) propagated along the real time-expanded transport graph and reweighted only by delivered records; wanted notices move at wire speed but only between offices, in office hours, and only where jurisdictions agree to act; watchers move by train. Hunters profile the quarry (class, hotels, banks, cable addresses), query ledgers where weight concentrates, or sit on fixed points the hypotheses must revisit, all within a budget. When weight collapses onto a city a cordon forms as a scheduled process over that city's exits, with formation curves, gaps (night shifts, branch halts, river piers, third-class exits) and budget-driven decay; the player reads its signs and consults the same timetable for the hole.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** P02.08's exact reachability makes hunters omniscient (its own risk note); P08.05's weights supply honest noise and profiling; P04.06 adds the wire, office-hours and liaison layer; P08.07 turns belief collapse into a rare, legible set-piece rather than a silent arrest.

### M-P08-3 — Comfort or Cover (the visibility dial)
- **Members:** P01.04, P09.09, P08.02, P04.07
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every purchasable service — carriage class, sleeping berth, lodging grade, payment instrument, message channel, cab — carries a cost vector of money, time, health, standing and trace. The route planner shows an exposure forecast beside departures: how many forced reveals (register, frontier, bank, cable) a plan contains, to which services, with what lag. The anonymous alternative always exists and always hurts — fatigue, illness, lost first-class society and rumours, a shabbiness that itself draws police attention — so the dial is tuned daily and no single profile dominates. Class gates conversation and business as well as visibility, so spending reads as investment, not leakage.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Three panelists converged on the same trilemma; combined it is the thirty-second decision the whole hunt hangs on and the honest reason a player cannot simply turtle.

### M-P08-4 — The Four Windows (what the player may know of the hunt)
- **Members:** P09.02, P09.10, P08.08, P08.09
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The player never sees hunter belief directly; they see it through four legitimate channels with their own costs and cadences. The calendar: institutional consolidation points — nightly register collection, weekly gazette, monthly circulars, tightening in wartime — are known in advance, their content never. The press: what hunters publish (descriptions, rewards, later photographs) is readable in the same editions and is also the player's counter-channel for planted sightings and agony-column traffic. The dossier: habit inferences with confidence levels, obtainable only by burglary, bribery or interception (P10.10's routine-mining feeds it). The autopsy: after capture or a survived near-miss, the full chain of records and inferences, replayable, with the branch points where it could have been broken.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** The pool's largest unsolved problem is the legibility of the hunt; these four windows are complementary, none is a minimap, and together they let forty hours of pursuit feel fair without exposing the model.

### M-P08-5 — Jurisdiction, Liaison and the Archive (the hunt's political geography and calendar)
- **Members:** P07.06, P03.06, P08.04, P02.11
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** There is no single hunter. Each service and private agency is bounded by legal reach (extradition treaties and the political-offence exception, consular jurisdiction, capitulations until 1914) and by liaison channels that open and close on the diplomatic calendar (anti-anarchist protocols 1898/1904, Entente sharing, 1917 collapse, the 1923 police commission). Dossiers are copied, inherited or lost as alliances form and regimes fall. Nothing archived is deleted: cable copies, registers and fingerprint cards persist, and macro-events (wartime censorship, a broken cipher class, a revolution seizing files) schedule reviews that resurface old records years later. Sanctuary is a resource the clock erodes; the player routes through seams, poisons channels, races a merger date, retires an identity in time or burns an archive.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Politics becomes the hunt's map rather than a separate screen, and the touchstone's "past catches up" arc emerges by mechanism; P08.12 folds into P02.11 and P08.04 into P03.06.

## Role-lens notes
Hunt fairness and depth audit. Fair means the hunter acts only on records that actually reached it and the player could, in principle, reconstruct why they were caught. By that test the pool is in good shape: P02.05, P04.05, P03.01, P08.01, P01.04, P02.08, P04.06, P08.05, P09.10, P10.10, P07.06 and P02.11 all derive the hunt from a delivered-evidence log. The outliers are P01.03's Heat account (fine as a display, dangerous as the thing hunters act on) and any suspicion accumulator not traceable to records; P07.08 passes because prescience is inferred from timestamped trades. P09.02's fixed cadence is honest but should emerge from institutional clocks (nightly Meldezettel, weekly gazette, monthly circulars) rather than a global metronome, or it reads as designed.

Compatibility: the trace models are strikingly interoperable. All assume one schema — subject legend, place, time, channel, fidelity, latency, persistence, reader set — so one hunter engine suffices: log, then per-service dossier filtered by reach (P02.05), then inference (reachability sets P02.08, weighted hypotheses P08.05, habit mining P09.10/P10.10), then actions (watchers P08.06, cordon P08.07, sitting on fixed points P10.03, liaison requests P04.06), then the player's windows (P09.02, P08.08, P09.10, P08.09). Item provenance (P02.07, P06.03), publication events (P06.08's gazettes, P08.08), rumour (P05.07) and archive reviews (P02.11) are the same log with other readers. P05.08 (interrogation generated from the hunters' evidence graph) and P06.08 (creditors as a second hunter with their own patience and information speed) slot in without new machinery.

Depth over forty hours needs three things the pool supplies separately and should keep together: a hunter that learns (P08.10 scepticism, P09.10 habits), a cast of hunters that changes with the calendar (P07.06, P08.04, P03.06) and a long fuse (P02.11, P08.12). Note for the Rapporteur: P05.09 and P06.01 share a title but are different concepts; P06.06 and P07.10 are the same concept.
