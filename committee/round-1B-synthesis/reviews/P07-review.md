# Round 1B-1 Review — Politics & Warfare Designer (Panelist P07)

## Overall reading of the pool
The pool converges hard on five load-bearing ideas, each reached independently by three or more panelists: hunters that read only institutional records (P01.04, P03.01, P04.05, P08.01, P08.05, P02.05); the race between a body on the timetable and news on the wire (P01.06, P02.02, P03.08, P04.06, P09.05); several paymasters each holding a belief model of the player (P01.07, P02.10, P05.09, P07.01, P09.11); legends as owned, maintained, burnable assets (P03.10, P05.01, P06.07, P08.03, P09.03, P10.02); and secrets as perishable, provenanced inventory (P01.08, P05.10, P06.04, P07.05). That convergence is the committee's real finding: the game is one ledger read by many institutions, and the hunt, the economy and the politics are different readers of it. The strongest whole-game hooks are the commerce-as-engine career (P03.12, P06.05, P06.06, P07.10), the bounded-history world with a computed inquest (P02.03, P07.02) and the war-era neutral hub (P03.11). What is thin relative to the directives is warfare as an observable, playable process (only P03.09 and P07.04), diplomacy as explicit crisis machinery (only P07.03), and faction agency — services and ministries that want things and plan (P02.12, P07.01, P07.09) — rather than politics as a passive price multiplier. The pool also leaves the player's own historical foreknowledge largely unaddressed, though P02.03, P03.04 and P03.08 name it as a replay risk and P07.08 prices it.

## Merge proposals (up to 5)
### M-P07-1 — Tramlines: Bounded History with an Inquest
- **Members:** P02.03, P07.02, P01.10, P07.08
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every world fact carries a tier. Tier 0 facts — the grounding kit's wars, revolutions, treaties and alliance entries — are exogenous rails with zero elasticity. Tier 1 facts (contract winners, crisis intensity and duration, line inaugurations, which service takes the credit) are windows jittered per playthrough and pushed by pressure accumulators that saturate. Tier 2 facts (arrests, exposures, local prices) are free. The runtime representation of pressure is P01.10's regional tension vector, which every fare, border-friction, document-value and payout lookup reads, so politics is felt without a politics screen, and influence actions are rare, expensive and lagged. The player's foreknowledge is priced: acting on 1914 before anyone else raises a per-faction prescience score that hunters treat as evidence of insider knowledge (P07.08), while Tier 1 jitter keeps textbook knowledge approximate. Because every state change carries provenance, the career ends in P02.03's computed inquest — a diff of the player's life against history, confined by construction to Tiers 1 and 2.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** P02.03 alone lets kit dates drift (a directive-1 risk); P07.02 alone has no runtime field and no payoff moment; P07.08 alone is a patch. Together they deliver accuracy, bounded agency, a legible price field and systems-generated closure from one data schema.

### M-P07-2 — Several Masters, One Calendar of Reckonings
- **Members:** P02.10, P01.07, P05.09, P07.01
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The player is on the books of several services and commercial principals at once. Each is a faction entity with a budget set by its ministry's annual vote, a requirements generator driven by its current strategic worries, and a belief store about each of the player's identities (facts with provenance and confidence). The player's product is the report, composed from held tokens — observed facts, documents, rumours, fabrications (P01.07) — and each delivery is scored for novelty and corroboration. Beliefs are compared only at reconciliation events: a service's own periodic audit (P05.09) and liaison events scheduled by the diplomatic calendar (P02.10) — 1904, 1907 and 1914 fuse channels, 1917 shatters one and leaks its files. Contradictions become suspicion scaled by confidence; a crossed threshold stops pay and turns the patron into a hunter holding everything the player ever told it (P09.11 is convergent). A service's successes raise its budget and therefore its counter-intelligence capacity, so the player funds the hunters who will one day read him (P07.01).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists wrote this system independently; the merge supplies the pieces each lacked — a player verb (report composition), a schedule of reckonings (liaison), a budget loop and a defection path into the hunt — on one belief-store data model.

### M-P07-3 — War Regime: Mobilisation, Requisition and the Neutral Bazaar
- **Members:** P03.09, P07.04, P03.11
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** War is a regime overlay with internal phases, not an event. On the Tier 0 schedule, mobilisation collapses the civilian timetable edge by edge as military flows claim the same graph; frontiers close, exit permits and passports appear, cables are cut, censors read the post, contraband lists and blockade economics (navicerts, the Netherlands Overseas Trust) make trade illegal and lucrative, and an identity's nationality decides internment, sequestration and call-up. Military movement remains observable as edge loads: train-watching networks count carriages and report through censored neutral post. The neutral capitals become the market where every service's buyers sit — a three-sided hunt (enemy counter-intelligence, suspicious paymasters, neutrality police who expel) under scarcity. The overlay's parameters are tables by date and region, so the Boer, Russo-Japanese and Balkan wars are smaller instances of the same machinery, and the 1914–18 neutral-hub scenario is the engine's first vertical slice.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** P03.09 supplies the physics, P07.04 the regime stack and identity consequences, P03.11 the playable arena; separately each is a layer, together they make warfare a lived process the player prepares for, trades inside and is hunted through.

### M-P07-4 — The Liaison Map: Dossiers That Travel with the Alliances
- **Members:** P08.04, P07.06, P02.05, P02.11
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Each service's dossier on each player identity is a filtered view of the game's event log — only evidence that reached that service (P02.05). Liaison channels between services are edges with latency, fidelity and political gating that open and close on dated protocols and alliances (P08.04); when an edge opens, facts copy across partially and late (P07.06), so a legend burned in Paris is safe in Petersburg until 1907 and not after. Archives never forget: cable copies, registers and fingerprint cards persist and are reviewed when a macro-event schedules it — wartime censors reading back copies, a cipher class breaking, a revolution seizing and publishing files (P02.11). Regime falls delete or mutate stores, opening windows of invisibility. The player can read a dossier by stealing it, thin it, poison a channel, or race a merger by becoming indispensable to the receiving service first. Hunters reason only over their own dossier, so the diplomatic calendar is literally the hunt's tectonic schedule.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists converged on "the alliance calendar is the hunt map"; one data model — provenance-tagged facts in faction stores, copied along gated edges, reviewed by scheduled triggers — serves all four and makes directive 1's world clock the direct cause of directive 4's hunt.

### M-P07-5 — The Broker's Board: Factions Post the Deals, the Agent Brokers Them
- **Members:** P02.12, P03.12, P06.06, P07.10
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Services, ministries, arms firms, banks and legations are goal-driven planners over the world state; when a plan needs a human broker, courier or thief, the engine emits a commission into a bid market where the player competes with NPC agents (P02.12). The richest commissions are concessions and contracts: multi-year project objects needing a registered company, a guaranteeing bank, a sovereign grant obtained through intermediaries and bakshish, and legation backing (P03.12), inside a granting polity whose stability converges to the kit's dates while its texture stays perturbable (P06.06). Espionage is the verb that wins deals — stolen tenders, bought clerks, planted insolvency stories — and the same intelligence sells twice, to the buyer and to a service with a stake in the grant. Who holds a concession is Tier 1; that oil is struck in Persia in 1908, that 1914 sequesters enemy concessions and 1917–18 expropriates Russia, is Tier 0 (P07.10). The hunt comes from rivals' inquiry agents, embarrassed Powers and the auditor who reads the firm's books. P06.05, P01.02 and P09.07 are convergent.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** The faction planners give the broker career endless, coherent, politically caused opportunities; the broker career gives the planners a player-facing fantasy the owner can picture; tier discipline keeps the whole inside history.

## Role-lens notes
World-clock integrity audit. The pool's faction and service models are compatible and should be unified: P01.03's credit and heat, P02.05's dossier-as-filtered-log, P02.10's and P05.09's belief stores with reconciliation, P07.01's budgets and requirement generators, P07.06's and P08.04's alliance-gated liaison edges, P08.05's hypothesis sets and P09.11's thresholds all reduce to one faction entity: a provenance-tagged fact store about each player identity, a budget and capacity, a requirements generator, and liaison edges opened and closed by the diplomatic calendar. P01.03's heat should be a derived summary over that store, not separate state; P08.05's hypothesis set is the same store's spatial projection.

Accurate yet perturbable: P07.02, P07.03, P07.11, P07.12, P01.10 (if saturating), P03.04, P03.09, P06.11, P09.04 and P10.11 treat kit events as exogenous rails and perturb only texture. Derailment risks: P02.03 (elastic macro dates), P02.12 (autonomous planners), P06.06 (free stability model), P05.07 and P05.10 (rumour and sales moving crises), and P01.10's influence actions if unsaturated. One remedy covers all: tier discipline — kit events have zero elasticity; every faction goal, pressure variable and influence action targets Tier 1 or 2; saturation is presented as institutional inertia, never as a wall.

Foreknowledge is the unsolved shared problem: P02.03, P03.04 and P03.08 name replay knowledge as a risk, and P07.08's prescience-as-evidence plus Tier 1 jitter is the only proposed answer; it should become the standard one.

Gaps the twenty should cover: warfare as observable process (P03.09, P07.04), diplomacy as crisis machinery (P07.03), and faction agency (P02.12, P07.01, P07.09); without them politics is only a price multiplier.
