# Round 1B-1 Review — Player Psychology & Retention Designer (Panelist P09)

## Overall reading of the pool
The pool converges with striking force on five structures, each proposed independently by four to six panelists: hunters read ledgers rather than the player (P01.04, P03.01, P04.05, P08.01, P08.02, P09.09); the body races its own description across two networks (P01.06, P02.02, P02.08, P04.06); legends are owned assets with their own heat, upkeep and death (P01.03, P03.10, P05.01, P06.07, P08.03, P09.03, P10.02); the agent serves several masters whose belief models are reconciled on the diplomatic calendar (P01.07, P02.10, P05.09, P07.01, P09.11); and the timetable is the turn (P01.01, P02.01, P04.02, P09.01). Convergence this strong answers directive 5 as much as the owner's hook question: one event log, read through institutional lenses, serves travel, money, deception, the hunt and the life at once. The strongest hooks are the ones where consequence is attributable afterwards and failure is granular — P08.09's autopsy, P08.03's "lose a name, not the game", P01.04's "which comfort betrayed you" — and the weakest are the dozen that reach for "variable-ratio reinforcement" as if uncertainty were a virtue in itself. Relative to the directives, conversation as a minute-to-minute verb is thin (P05.03 and P05.08 carry it almost alone), warfare is mostly weather rather than something the player touches (P03.09 excepted), and almost nobody describes the first ten minutes of play: onboarding into six deep systems is the pool's largest unaddressed retention risk.

## Merge proposals (up to 5)
### M-P09-1 — The Double Book: Serving Several Masters
- **Members:** P01.07, P05.09, P07.01, P02.10
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The player is on the books of several intelligence services and commercial principals at once. Each holds a belief model of the player (fact sets with confidence and attributed source) and posts requirements drawn from the world clock. After each episode the player composes reports from tokens held (observed facts, documents, rumours) plus fabrications; each principal pays for novelty and corroboration and periodically reconciles against its other sources, rival leaks and the press. The game keeps two books — what happened and what each service believes — and the player carries a consistency debt that grows with every lie and is paid down by making lies true. Liaison events scheduled by the diplomatic calendar (1904, 1907, the 1914 fusion of Entente counter-intelligence, the 1917 spill of Russian files) compare models; a contradiction becomes suspicion, and a crossed threshold turns a paymaster into a hunter holding everything the player ever told it. Victory is retirement with the ledgers never reconciled. (P09.11 converges and should be absorbed.)
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists arrived here independently; unified, it gives the whole game its spine (the touchstone career) on a single belief-store architecture, and its hook is honest: dread of the cross-check and relief at the paid report are caused by the player's own lies, never by a dice roll.

### M-P09-2 — Exits and the Second Agent
- **Members:** P10.12, P09.12, P09.04, P10.01
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The life is designed to end, and the player steers toward the ending. Three unsynchronised clocks run (world, body, hunt) across up to thirty years; an exit dashboard shows how near each ending is — retirement (unencumbered wealth in a stable currency, a clean name, a non-extraditing haven), exile, ruin, capture, turning hunter — with a live valuation of what would be kept at today's exchange, so retiring on roubles in 1916 is visibly a mistake before it is made. Playing on is always available and always tempting because the board escalates honestly with the world clock. Capture is never game over (P08.09 supplies the hinge: an autopsy of the evidence chain, then interrogation, remand, expulsion or sentence). On death or final capture the world does not reset: a successor or a new agent begins at the current date in a world that remembers — a two-subject file, frozen accounts, a codebook in an evidence vault — carrying only what the player knows about routes, codes and markets, plus whatever share of assets the succession rules allow.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Each part alone has a retention hole (early retirement, oppressive inevitability, permadeath churn); together they close the loop — fair failure, continuation, then a scored and chosen stopping point — and convert the touchstone's "past catches up" arc into a system without authoring an ending.

### M-P09-3 — The Dossier Mirror: Routine Is a Trail
- **Members:** P10.10, P09.06, P10.08, P09.10
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Personal and operational life share one scheduler: rent days, club nights, bill maturities, remittances to dependants, reports due, visa expiries and travel all write timestamped witness records. Each hunting service's inference runs over the records that reached it: periodicity detection, place clustering, habit profiling (always first class, the same cable address, Thursday bank visits), and observable obligations that predict presence — a bill maturing in Vienna, a registered lease, a remittance posted on the first of the month. Dependants and retainers are obligation entries that are at once assets and levers the hunters can pull. Regularity feeds legends and makes you catchable; irregularity preserves freedom and thins the legend. The player can obtain the dossier (burglary, a bribed registry clerk, an intercepted circular) and read exactly what is inferred, with confidence levels and gaps; then break the habits it names, lay false routine as a trap, or discharge an obligation — at a cost in plausibility, money and trust, knowing that a sudden break is itself an anomaly. P02.05 supplies the engine: the dossier is a filtered view of the event log.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** It makes the life sim load-bearing for the hunt and gives the player the rarest honest hook in the pool, self-knowledge as counter-surveillance: seeing yourself as the hunter sees you is agency at its purest, and it externalises the mastery curve (a veteran's dossier is thin).

### M-P09-4 — The Departure Is the Turn
- **Members:** P01.01, P04.02, P02.01, P09.01
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** No abstract turn. The world is a discrete-event simulation and the player's decision points are departures: actions are placed in the gaps between timetabled trains, steamers and mail collections, with slack as the real resource. The connection is the atomic unit of tension — the dwell at a junction where the player is stationary, visible and spending, and where hunters' wires catch up; delays are seeded distributions whose odds are shown before commitment. The diary is the scheduler's visible face (departures, rent due, an awaited cable, tonight's registration reaching the police), and time jumps to the next event that touches the player — minutes near a cordon, days at sea — with the diary always showing what will pass, so the queue is never opaque. Every pause the game offers (hotel register, sleeper berth) is placed in sight of a visible departure, so sessions end aboard a night train and resolve on load with the world advanced: a cliffhanger generated by the schedule. P01.09 (Three Clocks) is the pacing frame and P04.10 (The Long Leg) the sealed-session variant.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists converged on the timetable as turn structure; merged, it yields one cadence model from thirty seconds to the campaign and the strongest honest "one more leg" loop in the pool — Zeigarnik and goal-gradient pulls from booked departures, not from timers or nags.

### M-P09-5 — The Price of Comfort
- **Members:** P01.04, P08.02, P03.01, P04.05
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every service the world sells — class, berth, hotel, bank instrument, cable, cab — sits on a triangle of speed, cost and visibility, and every purchase writes a typed record (what it reveals, forwarding latency, fidelity, persistence, who may read it) into an institutional ledger with historically specific coverage: a Meldezettel reaching the Polizeidirektion within a day, a Russian hotel's passport desk, a Wagons-Lits berth sheet, a steamer passenger list, a cable copy book — and nothing at all in pre-1914 Britain, the period's great sanctuary asymmetry. Hunters never see the player, only arrived records. The route planner shows an exposure forecast beside the timetable (how many reveals, to which services, with what delay), and hardship accrues as life-sim debt: fatigue, illness, a shabby appearance that itself draws attention. (P08.01, P09.09 and P04.07 converge and should be absorbed.)
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** This is the pool's largest convergence (six panelists) and the micro-foundation of the hunt; unified, it makes every mundane purchase a consequential, attributable choice — the player always knows which comfort betrayed them — and it is the mechanism by which Scotland Yard's reveal becomes period paperwork.

## Role-lens notes
Hook honesty audit. Convincing — captivation argued from depth, agency and consequence: P08.09 (fairness attribution predicts persistence after loss), P08.05 (a hunter that is a mind, not a meter), P01.04 and P08.02 (attributable micro-choices felt every night), P08.03 (granular failure), P05.02 (deception as debt with maturities), P03.01 (a registration slip "in transit" as Zeigarnik tension between sessions), P10.10 (a dilemma that never resolves), P07.03 (an unstoppable ladder makes tension pure), P06.08 (hyperbolic discounting against a legible countdown), P04.02 and P01.09 (cadence and alignments), P02.02 (near misses from a legible model). Merely asserted: P02.12 ("coherent infinite content"), P07.11 ("presence at history"), P04.11 and P02.06 ("optimisation" and "planning pleasure"), P07.10 ("deal-making highs on an intermittent schedule"), P03.11. Drift toward gambling framing: about ten proposals cite variable-ratio reinforcement as a selling point — P03.09 ("a slot machine of partial information"), P02.01 ("an opaque queue"), P06.10, P06.04, P05.03, P01.02, P07.01, P03.12, P08.10 ("gambling-grade anticipation"). Where the uncertainty is caused by the player's own traces and is legible afterwards (P08.02; P08.10's timetable-checked decoys) the mechanism is honest; where it is an opaque queue or sampling noise it is a slot machine, and candidate texts should reframe it: anticipation must be earned by causality the autopsy can show. P10.06 (Vices) is the one place gambling is diegetic; acceptable only if the ledger cost is real and visible. P07.07's hidden guaranteed failure is honest only if extraction scoring is explicit from the first stage. The pool's strongest session structure and "one more…" loop: the timetable-as-turn cluster (M-P09-4) for minutes to hours, P01.09 and P10.11 for the campaign-scale "one more year", and the exits cluster (M-P09-2) for retention after loss — together, the honest answer to "addictive".
