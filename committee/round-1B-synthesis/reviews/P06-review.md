# Round 1B-1 Review — Economy, Business & Artifacts Designer (Panelist P06)

## Overall reading of the pool
The pool converges hard on five ideas, each proposed independently by three to six panelists: the hunter reads only records (traces, registers, cable copies, provenance chains, dossiers); the timetable is the turn; money is a set of period instruments; legends and households are costed assets; and the career is a commission or concession agent serving several masters. The strongest hooks sit where those meet: a firm whose books fund the spying and incriminate the spy, an item whose heat is whatever the inspecting institution has learned, a bourse that reprices only when the wire arrives. Relative to the directives, items and economy are well covered as evidence and as burn, but the demand side (who pays, why, and what rivals bid) appears in only three proposals (P02.12, P01.02, P09.07), terminal valuation of a life in two (P09.12, P10.12), corruption as a price system in one (P06.09) and the physical logistics of goods in three (P04.03, P06.12, P04.11); without those, an economy has customers, exits and prices only by assertion. Several panelists reused the same titles ("The Double Ledger", "The Concession Hunter"), which is itself a convergence signal the Rapporteur should exploit.

## Merge proposals (up to 5)
### M-P06-1 — The Concession House
- **Members:** P03.12, P07.10, P06.06, P06.01
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The player operates a registered commission and concession house, a legal person in a chosen jurisdiction (P06.01), that brokers the period's great grants and contracts (railways, oil, mining, timber, arms, wartime munitions) in the semi-sovereign lands and the war economies. A concession is a multi-year project object with political preconditions: a company, a guaranteeing bank, a sovereign grant obtained through intermediaries and bakshish, and legation backing (P03.12); a chain from introduction to court faction to finance house to embassy, run against rival Powers' agents (P07.10); a state machine from rumoured to voided, valued daily on the bourse against a simulated polity stability (P06.06). Espionage is the tool: stolen tenders, bribed clerks, intelligence sold to the services with a stake in the grant, which is what pays the house's bills. Every transaction writes a line in the firm's double-entry books; the public ledger must reconcile with the true one, and the books are the artifact that auditors, creditors and counter-intelligence read. The world clock renegotiates everything (1908 reopens Ottoman grants, 1914 sequesters enemy concessions, 1917 expropriates Russia); who wins a grant is the player's to perturb, that oil is struck in Persia in 1908 is not.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Three panelists converged independently on this career, the strongest evidence in the pool that it is the game; the ledger gives the convergent concept its missing hunt vector (the business's own records) and its missing economic constraint (spy income must be laundered into business income or it cannot be spent).

### M-P06-2 — Money Has a Body
- **Members:** P06.02, P03.04, P06.11
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Wealth is a portfolio of period instruments (gold coin, national notes, circular letters of credit with letters of indication, circular notes, bills of exchange, telegraphic transfers, bearer bonds, pawn tickets), each with weight, reach (where and in which hours it can be drawn), identity-binding (which legend must sign) and trace (what record a drawing or transfer leaves). The player composes a purse per journey and a treasury per legend. Fixed parities make pre-1914 accounting restful; then the world clock runs the Great Freeze: August 1914 moratoria and closed bourses, Trading with the Enemy sequestration keyed to the nationality of the legend that opened each account, Soviet bank nationalisation and safe inspections from December 1917, exchange decrees, the mark's death in 1923. The player hedges identity as much as currency, and the early-warning value of intelligence is measured in what gets out in time. Hunters read drawings, cable copies and serial numbers: the money trail is the hunt's second evidence stream.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Two panelists converged on the instrument model; coupling the micro (what is in your pocket at the frontier) to the macro (which regime will destroy it) makes money a travel problem, a deception problem and a politics problem at once.

### M-P06-3 — The Cost Side: Burn Rate, Debt and the Obligation Calendar
- **Members:** P01.05, P10.09, P06.08, P09.06
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Everything the player owns or pretends to be costs money on a calendar. Legends, households, cover addresses, retainers and sub-agents have a daily burn (P01.05); borrowing to bridge lumpy income creates instruments with maturities, rollovers at rising cost and jurisdictional enforcement (P10.09); every creditor, whether bank, moneylender, tailor, casino or the intelligence service that paid an advance, is an agent with patience, information and a method, from notarial protest to a gazetted bankruptcy that prints a legend's name (P06.08); and all of it, with deliveries, reports and visa expiries, sits on one obligation calendar that is the main planning surface and that hunters read, because dated obligations predict presence (P09.06). Default cascades: protest, suit, gazette, lost credit, club and household, then a service calling in its advance as a coerced mission. Fleeing a jurisdiction buys time against creditors' information lag, not freedom.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists converged on cost as the forcing function; separately each is a meter, together they are the second hunter and the second clock, and the reason "one more job" is rational rather than greedy.

### M-P06-4 — Provenance and Perishable Value
- **Members:** P06.03, P02.07, P01.08, P07.05
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every significant item (document, codebook, weapon, financial instrument, passport) is an entity with an append-only chain of custody: issued, bought, stolen, copied, forged, carried through a frontier at a time, pawned, planted. Its heat is not a stored stat but a lazy function: on inspection the chain is walked against what the inspecting institution has learned (a forger's arrest, a batch reported missing, a bank's stolen-bond circular), so an item is safe until the news reaches that frontier post. Its value is a curve per faction keyed to the political calendar, rising toward a crisis and collapsing at supersession or publication, with copies lowering exclusivity and raising exposure because copies carry marks. The player's verbs are sell, trade, plant, leverage, use, destroy or launder (re-sell through a cut-out, re-stamp, retype), each writing to the chain; the hunter's evidence stream is the same chain read backwards.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists converged; one schema serves the hunt (traces), the economy (price and laundering cost) and politics (whose demand), and P02.07's lazy evaluation makes it scale to a continent with no per-tick cost.

### M-P06-5 — Trading Ahead of the Wire, and Paying for It
- **Members:** P02.09, P03.08, P06.10, P07.08
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Prices on each bourse and in each city change only when news arrives there, and news travels through the cable graph, the Reuters, Havas and Wolff territories, censor desks and edition times with measurable latency (P03.08). Espionage puts the player hours or days upstream: a stolen tender, a tapped cable office or a bought clerk opens a trading window in bonds, arms shares, commodities, marine insurance and goods carried within luggage limits across tariff walls (P06.10). But every trade is a timestamped record a service can correlate with the news that followed (P02.09), and acting on the macro-calendar too early and too often accumulates a prescience score that reads as insider, plotter or provocateur (P07.08). Each bourse has depth: large orders move the price against the trader and draw inspectors, and sudden fortunes must be laundered through the house (M-P06-1). Profit is informational arbitrage bounded by exposure.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists converged on latency arbitrage; without P07.08 and depth limits it is the pool's largest money printer, with them it is the cleanest expression of "spy to trade, trade to spy".

## Role-lens notes
Economic integrity audit. A spy economy needs four legs: income, burn, credit and a terminal valuation. Income is modelled seriously only in the concession and commission cluster (P03.12, P07.10, P06.06, P06.05, P06.01) and the demand generators (P02.12, P01.02, P09.07), which say who pays and why; burn in P01.05, P06.07, P10.02, P10.03; credit and its clock in P10.09, P06.08, P09.06, P10.08; terminal valuation only in P09.12 and P10.12. A candidate list missing a leg has a broken loop: income without burn breeds turtling, burn without credit breeds death spirals, and without a valuation money is score, not stakes. P01.03's three accounts are a fine core-loop abstraction, but "cash" must be instrument-typed (P06.02, P03.04) or the money trail (bank drawings, cable transfers, the endorsement chains that link legends in P08.03) vanishes from the hunt.

Item models are compatible. P06.03, P02.07, P01.08, P07.05, P08.01, P04.05, P05.06 and P04.03 all reduce to an entity with an append-only event log; heat and value are lazy functions over that log evaluated against an institution's known facts (P02.07), and a dossier (P02.05) is a filtered view of the same log. Adopt that schema once.

Where economies break: latency arbitrage (P02.09, P03.08, P06.10, P01.06) is unbounded without per-bourse depth, trades-as-evidence and P07.08's prescience penalty; plant-and-trade (P07.12, P01.11, P09.05 with P02.09) lets the player front-run his own rumours; double-selling secrets (P06.04, P05.10, P07.05) needs pay-on-corroboration and copy marks; burning a legend to shed its debts (P06.08, P10.09, P09.03) is dominant unless private inquiry agents (P08.04) pursue across names; P09.12's score must weigh legend cleanliness and file size or cashing out early wins; bribery (P06.09) needs detection that scales with frequency. For scale, markets should be functions of arrived facts, never tick simulations.
