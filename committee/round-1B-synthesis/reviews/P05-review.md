# Round 1B-1 Review — Deception, Conversation & Cryptography Designer (Panelist P05)

## Overall reading of the pool
The pool converges, from ten different lenses, on one architecture: every act writes a typed record into an institutional ledger (hotel slip, cable copy, bank counterfoil, frontier stamp) that reaches factions with latency, and hunters, creditors and paymasters reason only from what has reached them (P08.01, P02.05, P04.05, P03.01, P01.04, P09.02). On that substrate three deception clusters appear almost identically in four or five files each: legends as weighted fact-graphs that can be fed, linked and burned (P10.02, P03.10, P08.03, P09.03, P05.01); serving several masters with per-faction belief models reconciled at dated liaisons (P07.01, P02.10, P01.07, P05.09, P09.11); and messages as copied, censored, archived objects (P03.02, P03.03, P02.11, P05.04). The strongest hooks are those where deception and the hunt are literally one system — feeding the hunter manufactured records (P08.10), burning a legend to collapse a fix (P09.03), racing your own description (P01.06, P02.02, P04.06), the archive that re-reads old cables years later (P02.11, P08.12). What is missing, relative to directive 3, is conversation itself: outside P05.03, P05.08 and P06.09's bribery, no proposal gives face-to-face exchange states, resources or consequences, and nothing offers the player a mechanism (rather than an assertion) for estimating what an NPC or a service believes. Forgery as a supply system (P05.06) and human-source recruitment are similarly thin, while timetable, money, hunt and life-arc systems are abundantly and compatibly covered.

## Merge proposals (up to 5)
### M-P05-1 — Legends as Falsifiable Record-Sets
- **Members:** P10.02, P03.10, P08.03, P09.03
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** A legend is a first-class entity that owns a fact-graph (name, nationality, profession, residence, bank, references, papers). Each fact carries an evidentiary weight that decays unless fed by real acts under that name — a hotel slip, an account drawing, a club subscription, a residence-office card — and a verification cost in days and money for any hunter who audits it through period channels (commercial inquiry agency, consular register, professional directory, residence office). Every act writes a witness record (legend × circle × city × date); hunters keep one dossier per legend, not per player, and advance only through linkage events (shared handwriting, one trunk under two names, a draft endorsed across names, a face matched across files). The player sees legends as separate heat columns with a linkage-risk indicator and keeps them timetable-incompatible. A legend can be rested (cheap, decaying), retired cleanly (slow, expensive), sold as an item with provenance, or burned — instantly collapsing a hunter's fix into pursuit of a ghost while forfeiting its accounts, households and standing. Compatible siblings to fold in: P05.01 (verification latency computed from the communications and travel graphs), P06.07 (lifestyle burn rate), P02.04 and P04.12 (concurrent lives held apart by the timetable), P04.09 (itinerary plausibility), P01.03 (heat per legend).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists specified the same data model independently from the life-sim, archival, pursuit and retention sides; merged, one entity is at once the hunt's target, the economy's cost centre, travel's alibi constraint and the player's most-loved possession.

### M-P05-2 — Several Masters, One Truth
- **Members:** P07.01, P02.10, P01.07, P05.09
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The player is on the books of several services and commercial principals at once. Each holds a belief store of typed facts (subject, predicate, value, confidence, source, time) about the world and about the player; the player's product is the report, composed from tokens held (observed facts, documents, intercepts, rumours) plus fabrications, and paid for by novelty and corroboration. Stores are compared only at reconciliation events — liaison channels opened by the alliance calendar (1904, 1907, the 1914 Entente fusion, the 1917 file spills), cross-checks against newspapers and registers, a rival's leak — so computation is bounded and reckonings are dated and partly foreseeable. Contradictions yield suspicion scaled by the product of confidences; the player carries a consistency debt paid down by making lies true (planting the register entry, arranging the event). When a belief flips to "double", the paymaster becomes a hunter holding everything the player ever told it as a dossier. Siblings: P09.11, P07.06 (dossiers travelling with alliances), P06.01 (the commercial two-books variant).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** P07.01 and P05.09 give the whole-game shape, P02.10 the exact bounded computation, P01.07 the player-facing verb of composing the report; together the craft of consistent lying has a resource, a clock, a verb and an ending.

### M-P05-3 — Codes, Ciphers, Censors and Copies
- **Members:** P03.02, P03.03, P05.05, P09.08
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every message is an object with readers. A cable exists three times and its office copy is a dormant item obtainable under warrant or read routinely from August 1914; brevity is bought with commercial code books (readable by anyone holding the edition) or private cipher (legal before 1914 but conspicuous, then admitted only from deposited lists). Letters pass adversarial reading offices — Russian black cabinets before the war, every belligerent's censorship after — whose anomaly heuristics run over structured components (open text, open code, secret ink, routing via cover address) and improve by date, so codes have half-lives and must rotate. Intercepts the player obtains are real ciphertexts: decode by hand (free, private, slow; a skill that grows and rusts), with a codebook item (fast; an incriminating artifact with provenance), through a bureau or hired cryptanalyst (fast, costly, leaves a copy), or by abstract roll — all rungs yielding the same intelligence object. A captured book reads a traffic class until its owner rotates it, triggered by a hidden compromise-suspicion value raised by visible exploitation: the burn-the-source wager. Siblings: P05.04 (routing chooses readers; messages race the traveller), P02.11 (archived traffic decrypted years later), P01.12 (codes as the price of delegation).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** P03's procedures make coding accurate and institutional, P09's staircase makes it a mastery curve, P05's rotation and source-burning give it strategic consequence; coding becomes a system with states rather than a word minigame.

### M-P05-4 — Papers: Documents as Composed, Regulated, Provenanced Items
- **Members:** P05.06, P03.05, P02.07, P06.03
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every document is a field-structured item (stock, typeface, seal, signature, serial, stamps, photograph, dates) whose fields carry quality grades and whose provenance chain (issued, stolen, altered, stamped at a frontier, pawned, planted) is append-only. Every travel edge carries a dated document requirement from the regime graph — none, passport, visa, exit permit, photograph — that flips with the world clock (Russian and Ottoman regimes before 1914; the decrees of August 1914; photographic passports 1915; the 1920 standard booklet; Nansen certificates 1922). Inspectors have depth profiles per institution and year — a gendarme glances at a seal, a consulate wires the issuing register — resolved field by field with partial feedback to the player. A document's safety is a live property computed from what the world has learned: a forger's arrest turns every paper from his hand hot at every post the news has reached. Forgery is crafting from components (stock, seals, specimen signatures, typewriters with identifiable wear) against predicted inspection depth; altering genuine papers has its own failure modes; both are provenance editing. Sibling: P04.04 (the frontier station as decision node).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** The regime graph is the demand, the bench the supply and provenance the consequence — one object type serving travel, deception, economy (letters of credit and bills are documents) and the hunt.

### M-P05-5 — The Exchange: Claims, Calls, Prices and Pitches
- **Members:** P05.03, P05.02, P06.09, P05.08
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** One negotiation engine for every face-to-face act. Parties hold Composure, Rapport, Leverage and an Agenda and play Assert, Probe, Offer, Press, Concede, Deflect and Call; a Call demands evidence (documents, witnesses, timetable facts), bluffs resolve against suspicion and composure, and NPC tell profiles are learnable over repeated exchanges. Every Assert the player makes becomes a liability in a claims ledger with a holder and a maturity date — the moment the world's own clock will test it — to be serviced (made true), rolled over (a supporting lie) or bought back (partial confession); defaulted lies become leads. Bribery is the same game against a hidden reservation price and preferred form of payment; recruitment is a pitch sized to a hidden motivation profile built by investigation; interrogation is the game played from the other chair against a dossier the player must infer from question patterns. Siblings: P10.04 (standings as the memory conversation writes to), P07.09 (networks built by recruitment), P08.09 (interrogation as the act after capture), P10.08 (obligations as leverage lines).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** The pool contains no other conversation system; fusing bribery, recruitment, interrogation and the lie ledger into one engine makes conversation a deep system with resources, states and a clock, and gives the belief-model proposals (P02.10, P07.01, P09.10) the in-world probe they currently lack for reading what others believe.

## Role-lens notes
Deception, conversation and coding are real systems — with states, inputs and consequences — in P02.10 (belief tuples reconciled at liaison), P01.07 (reports composed from tokens; consistency checker), P02.05 (dossier as filtered log), P08.10 (records with hidden authorship; scepticism learner), P08.03/P07.06 (per-legend dossiers copied along alliance edges), P10.02/P03.10/P05.01 (weighted fact-graphs audited with cost and lag), P03.02/P03.03 (copies; censor heuristics with half-lives), P09.08/P05.05 (real ciphers), P06.09 (hidden reservation price), P10.04/P05.07 (standings and rumours on circle graphs), and P05.03, P05.02, P05.08. Elsewhere "deception" appears only as coverage and depends on these clusters existing.

Compatibility: the pool uses four representations that are one schema under four indexes. (a) A fact tuple (subject, predicate, value, confidence, source, time): P02.10's beliefs, P10.02's facts, P05.01's claims, P01.07's tokens, P05.02's lies (a tuple whose value differs from the world's, plus a maturity). (b) An evidence record in an append-only log with channel, latency, fidelity and access: P08.01, P04.05, P03.01, P02.05, P09.02. (c) A faction store = a filtered view of that log plus claims asserted to it, linked over identity tags: P02.05, P08.03, P07.06, P09.10. (d) Provenance = facts attached to objects: P06.03, P02.07, P01.08. Legends own (a); the world emits (b); hunters hold (c); items carry (d). Adopt P02's tuple as canonical, with "verification" a scheduled query, "liaison" a store merge, "forgery" a tuple written into a document field and "rumour" a tuple mutating on propagation; every cluster then interoperates without new types.

Gaps: an in-world probe for what NPCs and factions believe (many proposals say "estimated, imperfectly" without a mechanism; the Exchange's Call and tells supply one); human-source recruitment and handling (only P05.08, P07.09); forgery supply (only P05.06). My P05.04, P05.07 and P05.10 are subsumed by M-P05-3, P10.04/P07.12/P08.08 and P07.05/P01.08/P06.04 respectively; P05.11 and P05.12 are risky extras viable only once this schema exists.
