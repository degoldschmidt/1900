# Round 1B-1 Review — Core Loop & Systems Designer (Panelist P01)

## Overall reading of the pool
The pool converges far more than ten independent panelists had any right to: the same loop was proposed from eight lenses — the timetable is the turn (P01.01, P02.01, P04.02, P09.01); every convenience is paid for in trace (P01.04, P04.07, P08.02, P09.09); hunters reason only from institutional records propagated on the real timetable and the real wire (P02.05, P02.08, P03.01, P04.05, P04.06, P08.01, P08.05); legends are owned, fed, burnable assets with their own heat (P01.03, P03.10, P05.01, P08.03, P09.03, P10.02); several paymasters hold belief models reconciled at liaison (P01.07, P02.10, P05.09, P07.01, P09.11); goals are generated, windowed and geographically exclusive (P01.02, P02.12, P07.05, P09.07); and debt is a second clock and a second hunter (P01.05, P06.07, P06.08, P10.09). The strongest hooks are the ones that bind two directives in a single number — a cost vector with a trace component, a value curve with a holding risk, a legend with a heat column — because those are what make the systems one game. What is thin relative to the directives is *conversation* as a first-class verb (only P05.03 and P05.08 treat it as a system rather than a conduit) and the *player's own instruments*: nearly every risk line in the pool says "must be legible without being a spreadsheet", yet only P02.05, P08.01, P09.10 and P08.09 propose what the player actually reads about their own exposure. The Historian's errata (change of train at the gauge break; Trans-Siberian through running 1903–04; no British hotel registration before 1914–16) touch only dates and wording in the pool, not mechanics; P04.01 and P04.08 should read "Circum-Baikal 1904".

## Merge proposals (up to 5)
### M-P01-1 — The Timetable Is the Turn
- **Members:** P01.01, P02.01, P04.02, P09.01
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** The world is a discrete-event simulation whose visible face is the diary and the timetable (P02.01). The player's decision points are departures (P01.01); the atomic unit of tension is the connection — the gap between arrival and next departure, when the player is stationary, visible and spending (P04.02); and every pause the game offers (hotel, berth, safe house) sits within sight of a departure, so sessions end aboard trains rather than in menus (P09.01). Every verb — meet, cable, forge, trade, bribe, register — is an action with a duration placed into those gaps; slack is the resource actually managed; the queue advances to the next event that touches the player, so a quiet winter passes in minutes and a closing cordon takes an hour.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** Four panelists converged; together they supply the engine (the queue), the loop (departure as turn), the tension unit (the connection) and the session shape (pause before departure) as one mechanism instead of four pitches.

### M-P01-2 — Pay for Silence
- **Members:** P08.02, P01.04, P04.07, P09.09
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Every purchasable service — train class, berth, hotel grade, payment instrument, communication channel, cab — carries one cost vector: money, time, health, standing, trace (P09.09). The trade is speed, comfort and society against the records left at the period's forced-disclosure points — register, frontier, bank, cable (P08.02). Class also buys access: first class opens conversation with officials and businessmen, so anonymity costs opportunity, not just comfort (P04.07). The route planner shows an exposure forecast beside the fare (P08.02); hardship accumulates as life-sim debt — illness, missed appointments, a shabbiness that itself draws police attention — so neither "always first class" nor "always sleep rough" dominates (P01.04, P09.09). The hunter consumes only the trace component, never ground truth.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** This is the thirty-second decision the whole game is made of; four statements of it become one cost-vector interface that the hunt, the economy and the life-sim all read from the same field.

### M-P01-3 — Wants, Windows and the Jobs the World Posts
- **Members:** P01.02, P02.12, P09.07, P07.05
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** No authored missions. Factions derive goals from world state and, when a plan needs a human courier, thief, broker or forger, post a commission for which the player is one candidate among NPC agents (P02.12). The board fills with located, time-windowed offers made mutually exclusive by travel time, and lapsed offers report their outcomes (P09.07). Beside the wants sit windows — the places and intervals where the wanted thing is obtainable — and the loop is matching window to want across the graph before either closes; a want with no window can be met by forgery (P01.02). Every secret carries a per-faction value curve that rises toward the relevant crisis and collapses at supersession or publication, and holding it is evidence (P07.05), so timing, routing and exposure are decided in one move.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** The parts supply the generator (faction planners), the pacing (windows, exclusivity), the valuation (curves) and the hunt coupling (holding risk, provenance); together they are a complete goal system with no quest log.

### M-P01-4 — The Second Clock: One Ledger, Burn and Debt
- **Members:** P01.03, P06.07, P06.08, P10.09
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Each legend keeps one ledger with three balances — cash in period instruments, credit with institutions, heat held by each service on that name — and every action moves at least two (P01.03). Each live legend has a monthly burn matched to its class and household (P06.07; P01.05 is the same mechanism), and every borrowing is an instrument with a maturity and a jurisdictional enforcement regime, so the calendar fills with due dates that run independently of the world clock (P10.09). Default is a public act — a protested bill, a gazetted bankruptcy that prints the legend's name — so creditors are a second hunter whose information crosses cities at letter or cable speed (P06.08). Burning a legend discards its heat and its debts together, which tempts deliberate fraud and is priced accordingly; currency regimes (1914, 1917, 1923) are debt events.
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** It turns the economy from a score into a clock and a pursuer and binds it to the legend portfolio, so "why go out and be seen" is answered by the system rather than by a mission list.

### M-P01-5 — The Information Front
- **Members:** P02.02, P01.06, P04.06, P09.05
- **Merged concept (one paragraph):** Two graphs: the time-expanded transport network the player's body rides, and the communication network — cable, post, wireless, rumour, editions — along which facts travel at their medium's latency (P02.02, P01.06). A revelation about an identity propagates office to office, in office hours, only across jurisdictions with a liaison agreement (P04.06); the player sees an estimated front and races it — a night train outruns tomorrow's newspaper but not tonight's wire; cross the frontier before the warrant is relayed; change the thing described. The same layer is the player's own window on the world: events are seen only through lagged channels, acting on stale news is the core risk, and knowing which city hears what first is mastery (P09.05).
- **Why the merge is stronger than the parts:** One propagation layer serves the hunters' knowledge, the player's feedback, the wanted-notice press mirror and, if wanted, the news-latency markets (P03.08, P02.09); three panelists proposed the race independently and P09.05 supplies its player-facing half.

## Role-lens notes
Coherence audit. One loop is present in the pool and it reads like this: the timetable is the clock (M-P01-1); burn and debt force the player to earn (M-P01-4); the world posts windowed, exclusive wants (M-P01-3); every verb used to meet them — travel, converse, forge, cipher, bribe, trade — spends the same cost vector of money, time, health, standing and trace (M-P01-2); records land in institutional ledgers with provenance (P08.01, P04.05, P03.01, P02.07, P06.03); hunters reason only from those ledgers, along the same timetable, at wire speed between offices (P08.05, P02.08, M-P01-5), on a known cadence (P09.02) that ends in a cordon (P08.07); legends are the buffer that makes loss granular (P09.03, P08.03, P10.02); paymasters' belief models reconcile at liaison dates (P05.09, P02.10, P07.01, P09.11), so politics is the hunt's origin; bounded history (P07.02, P02.03, P07.03) moves every parameter on schedule; archives reopen (P02.11, P08.12) and the autopsy (P08.09) closes each loss as a lesson. The common interfaces are three: P02.01's event queue as the only clock, P09.09's cost vector as the only price, P08.01's record as the only evidence. Any proposal that cannot express itself in those three is a minigame — which is my test for P05.05/P09.08 (passes only via the ladder), P05.03 (passes only if every Assert writes to P05.02's claims ledger), P05.06 (passes if it reuses frontier inspection depth), P02.09/P06.10 (bounded income), P06.12, P08.11 and P05.11 (fail as standalones). The strongest single core loop is M-P01-1 through M-P01-5 plus the hunter, legends, masters and tiers clusters, with P10.11's annual audit as the campaign-scale beat and P10.12/P09.04 as the exits.
