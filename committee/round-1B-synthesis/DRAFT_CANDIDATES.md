## Procedure and tally

### How (a)–(f) were applied

- **(a) Support.** Support per proposal is the Secretary's count of distinct nominating panelists (67 proposals nominated; 100 nominations).
- **(b) Clusters.** The fifty merge proposals were grouped where two merges share at least two member IDs or plainly describe one concept (the four identical "Departure Is the Turn" merges; the three "Several Masters" merges; the two "Exits" merges; the three "Jurisdiction/Liaison/Archive" merges; the three "Papers/Provenance" merges; the three "Routine" merges; the three "Debt/Burn" merges). A cluster's support is the number of distinct panelists who nominated any member — never a sum. Where a chain of merges joined distinct hooks (the hunt chain linked by M-P09-5 and M-P04-2; the history–war chain linked by M-P02-3; the papers–provenance chain linked by M-P06-4), the cluster was split by hook along the panel's own carving: an evidence/record engine (C17), hunters that reason and move on the timetable (C05), the visibility dial (C02), the player's windows on the hunt (C16), the liaison map (C03), and the routine-mining layer (C14); bounded history (C10) against the crisis/war regime (C09). Each proposal was homed in exactly one candidate for support counting (members); where a merge or audit also used its text elsewhere it is listed as "draws on" and not counted. Two concept merges were made across clusters because the panel's own texts and flags ask for them: the money-instrument cluster (M-P06-2) joined the ledger/debt cluster on P06's audit instruction that P01.03's cash "must be instrument-typed" (C08); the news-latency market (M-P06-5) joined the Information Front on P01's flag and the explicit text of M-P02-2 and M-P03-2 (C13). The body/health cluster (M-P10-2) joined the dial cluster by the ≥2-member rule (it shares P09.09 and P08.02).
- **(c) Ranking.** Clusters were ranked by support. Ties were broken toward broader directive coverage (number of the eight areas — travel, deception/conversation/coding, items, economy, politics/warfare, time pressure, the hunt, the life — a candidate touches as primary or secondary), then toward diversity of supporting roles, then by number of panelists proposing the merge, then by total nominations of members, then by the lead proposal's own support. Nineteen clusters have support ≥3; the twentieth slot was decided among three clusters at support 2 (see Adjustments). The twenty were then checked pairwise for distinct central hooks.
- **(d) Flags.** All 46 flag rows (25 flagged proposals) were applied as the flaggers propose: duplicates merged and counted once; infeasible proposals bounded and folded as options; the directive-1 risk in P02.03 fixed by tiering (Tier-0 dates immovable; jitter and pressure only on Tiers 1–2; DAG and inquest kept); P03's historical corrections written into the texts; the "Double Ledger" / "Double Book" title collisions resolved by renaming. No flag was rejected.
- **(e) Pitches.** Each candidate is written as a unified, playable game around its nucleus, drawing on the merged texts and the source proposals' own mechanics; no persons, plots, scenes, art or tone.
- **(f) Breadth.** Checked after ranking; adjustments and reasons recorded below.

## Candidates

## C01 — Several Masters, One Truth
- **Type:** whole-game concept
- **One-line pitch:** Several services and commercial principals pay you at once, each holding its own model of you; retire before those models are reconciled.
- **Central hook:** Each paymaster is a faction with a belief store of facts about you; your report is composed from held tokens and fabrications; stores are compared only at dated reconciliation events, and contradiction turns a patron into a hunter.
- **How it plays:** Minute to minute: take each service's requirements, decide who gets which fragment and who gets chickenfeed. Session: deliveries scored for novelty and corroboration; lies booked as consistency debt, payable by making them true. Campaign: the alliance calendar schedules reckonings — 1907 opens channels between employers, 1914 fuses Entente counter-intelligence, 1917 leaks a fallen service's files; a belief flipping to "double" stops pay and starts the hunt.
- **Directive coverage:** Politics and deception/coding primary; the hunt at the end. Secondary: economy (retainers), travel (handlers in several capitals), items (reports, plans), time (liaison dates), the life (accounts over decades).
- **Why captivating & addictive:** Sustained dramatic irony; near-misses you controlled; each added master is a self-set difficulty slider; betrayal emerges from your own inconsistencies, so dread and relief are honest.
- **Engine implications:** One faction entity: a provenance-tagged fact store per identity, budget, requirements generator and alliance-gated liaison edges; reconciliation only at liaison or audit events; suspicion scales with the product of confidences; token-based composition, never free text.
- **Historical grounding:** Secret Service Bureau (1909), Deuxième Bureau, Evidenzbureau, Abteilung IIIb, Okhrana foreign bureau; Entente liaison 1914–18; Okhrana files seized, partly burned, partly published in 1917.
- **Provenance:** P07.01 (lead), P02.10, P05.09, P01.07, P09.11; M-P05-2, M-P07-2, M-P09-1; draws on P07.06 (C03), P06.01 (C15).
- **Support:** 7 (P01, P02, P03, P05, P07, P09, P10); merge proposed by P05, P07, P09.
- **Flags addressed:** P09.11 (duplicate ×3) and P01.07 (duplicate) absorbed, keeping consistency debt; P05.09 title collision resolved by renaming.

## C02 — Comfort or Cover
- **Type:** game-defining system (pitched as a game)
- **One-line pitch:** Every convenience is registered and every anonymous alternative hurts: each night you choose between the sleeper and the register.
- **Central hook:** Every purchasable service — class, berth, lodging, instrument, channel, cab — carries one cost vector: money, time, health, standing, trace; the four forced-disclosure points (register, frontier, bank, cable) harvest the trace, and the body pays for the quiet end.
- **How it plays:** Thirty seconds: pick class, hotel, instrument and channel; the planner shows an exposure forecast beside the fare. Day: first class buys officials' conversation and writes your name into lists; third class and gold coin write nothing and cost sleep. Season: hardship accrues as health stocks (fatigue, disease, slow wounds) and a shabbiness that draws police attention; drink and cards relieve stress at compounding cost.
- **Directive coverage:** Travel, the hunt and the life (health, ageing) primary. Secondary: economy (price of anonymity), deception (class matches legend), items (coin against instruments), politics (requisition), time (bank hours, convalescence).
- **Why captivating & addictive:** Approach-avoidance conflict: you want the berth and fear the register; consequences stay attributable (you know which comfort betrayed you); loss aversion on health and cover means no profile dominates.
- **Engine implications:** Service entries carry (duration, price, visibility vector) plus health and standing deltas; trace records consumed only through C17; health as stocks with timers and per-city seasonal exposure tables; vice stocks (craving, tolerance).
- **Historical grounding:** Three classes and Wagons-Lits supplements; hotel registration in Germany, Austria, France and Russia, none in Britain before 1914–16; no antibiotics; Salvarsan 1910; influenza 1918–20.
- **Provenance:** P08.02 (lead), P01.04, P09.09, P04.07, P10.07, P10.06; M-P01-2, M-P08-3, M-P09-5 (dial half), M-P10-2.
- **Support:** 5 (P01, P04, P08, P09, P10); merge proposed by P01, P08, P09, P10.
- **Flags addressed:** none on members; P10.06 kept mechanical per P09's audit (gambling diegetic only, ledger cost visible).

## C03 — The Liaison Map: Jurisdiction, Alliances and the Archive
- **Type:** game-defining system (pitched as a game)
- **One-line pitch:** No single hunter: each service is bounded by law and by liaison channels that open and close on the diplomatic calendar; nothing archived is deleted.
- **Central hook:** Independent hunters bounded by extradition treaties and consular courts; dossiers copy along alliance edges with delay, pass partially to successor regimes and reopen under archive reviews — the world clock decides who knows what, when.
- **How it plays:** Daily: route through the seams — a Sûreté lead dies at the frontier unless Paris cables Brussels; London is sanctuary until wartime alien registration. Yearly: watch realignments and race a merger — thin the file, become indispensable to the receiving service, or retire the identity. Decades: a 1902 fingerprint card or a 1916 cable copy decrypted in 1919 resurfaces; pre-empt by burning registers, choosing courier over cable.
- **Directive coverage:** Hunt and politics primary. Secondary: deception (identity hygiene), travel (seams are frontiers), items (the file), economy (inquiry agents), time (merger dates, long fuse), the life (ageing faces).
- **Why captivating & addictive:** Foreseeable dread: you know the date your past arrives; a readable geography of safety shifting every few game-years; catharsis when a collapse wipes a slate or the last copy burns.
- **Engine implications:** N faction stores (C17 views) with a liaison graph and a cooperation/extradition matrix (service × jurisdiction × date); archive nodes store event ids, not copies; macro-events schedule reviews with per-archive destruction fractions.
- **Historical grounding:** Extradition Act 1870, no Anglo-Russian treaty; Ottoman capitulations to 1914; Okhrana Paris bureau; Okhrana abolished March 1917; Vienna commission 1923.
- **Provenance:** P08.04 (lead), P03.06, P07.06, P02.11, P08.12; M-P03-4, M-P07-4, M-P08-5.
- **Support:** 5 (P03, P05, P07, P08, P10); merge proposed by P03, P07, P08.
- **Flags addressed:** P07.06 (P03): partial archive survival, no institutional Cheka inheritance; P02.11/P08.12 (P03 audit): retrospective reading is a rare review event.

## C04 — The Legend Portfolio: Several Lives at Once
- **Type:** whole-game concept
- **One-line pitch:** Legends are the unit of account: they keep running in other cities whether or not you wear them, and the hunt advances only by linking two names.
- **Central hook:** A legend is an entity owning a fact-graph whose weights decay unless fed by acts under that name, with its own residence, accounts, debts and burn; hunters keep one dossier per legend and advance only through linkage events.
- **How it plays:** Daily: every act under a name — hotel slip, account drawing — deepens its record-set and writes a witness record. Weekly: plan a circuit so unattended lives do not go stale; a linkage-risk indicator warns before you arrive as A on B's ticket. Crisis: burn a legend to collapse a hunter's fix into pursuit of a ghost, forfeiting its accounts.
- **Directive coverage:** The life, deception and the hunt primary. Secondary: travel (verification latency is routing), items (papers, labels), economy (upkeep; name-bound credit), politics (nationality: internment from 1914), time (records age).
- **Why captivating & addictive:** IKEA and endowment effects: burning a legend nurtured for twenty hours bites without killing; granular failure (lose a name, not the game) keeps players playing.
- **Engine implications:** Identity entity with components; unattended identities run at low detail and materialise deterministically on arrival; identity-tagged evidence with a link-detection pass; fact graphs audited by hunter queries with cost and lag.
- **Historical grounding:** Meldewesen; Britain unregistered before 1915 (where people are invented), Germany with resident cards (where inventions die); internment staged 1914–15; photographic passports February 1915.

## C19 — Everything That Moves Has an Itinerary
- **Type:** whole-game concept
- **One-line pitch:** The timetable that moves you also moves your luggage, crates, couriers, letters, sub-agents, each with an itinerary; your plans compile into the world's scheduler.
- **Central hook:** Any entity holds an itinerary; an operation is a task DAG with windows and fallbacks that the engine validates against the timetable, compiles and re-plans when the world intrudes; a compromised node cascades through all in flight.
- **How it plays:** Pack: hand luggage is searched at frontiers; registered luggage goes ahead by slow freight; left-luggage receipts are tradable keys. Consign: a crate under a false manifest moves at freight speed, inspected with odds shaped by plausibility and bribes. Dispatch: couriers and sub-agents act by cable and post, reporting late, garbled or, if turned, false. Presence is fast and visible; delegation slow and corruptible; decide where your body goes.
- **Directive coverage:** Travel, items and economy primary. Secondary: hunt (cargo is a record; the network is traced), deception (manifests), coding (code books for delegation), politics (contraband, blockade), time (cargo never sleeps).
- **Why captivating & addictive:** Planning pleasure, flow when repairing under pressure; ownership of a growing machine; cascading failures are crises of your own making; the customs gamble is a repeated micro-climax.
- **Engine implications:** Declarative plan DAGs; critical path and slack on the timetable; one planner for all agents; itinerary-holder entities; examination as a rule table (edge, date, papers, manifest); agents with loyalty and message queues.
- **Historical grounding:** Weight-based excess charges; sealed through luggage; grande and petite vitesse goods classes; bills of lading and Lloyd's insurance; transshipment ports; 1914 gun-running.
- **Provenance:** P02.06 (lead), P04.03, P04.11, P06.12, P01.12; M-P02-4, M-P04-4; draws on P06.10's luggage trade loop (C13).
- **Support:** 3 (P02, P04, P06); merge proposed by P02, P04.
- **Flags addressed:** P06.12 (P01: scope) bounded — cargo writes records into C17's ledger, no separate hunter board; the body stays central.

## C20 — Codes, Ciphers, Censors and Copies
- **Type:** game-defining system (pitched as a game)
- **One-line pitch:** Every message has readers: a cable's office copy outlives you, letters pass learning censors, and intercepts are ciphertexts you break by hand, book, bureau or roll.
- **Central hook:** An abstraction ladder (hand, book, bureau, roll) yields one object; censors score letter components with date-improving heuristics, so codes have half-lives; a captured book reads traffic until its owner rotates it when visible exploitation raises hidden suspicion.
- **How it plays:** Composing: shave words (cables cost per word); commercial code anyone with the edition reads, or private cipher, admitted only from deposited lists after August 1914; route by courier for permanence over speed. Reading: frequency and crib tools make Playfair feasible by hand; novices pay a bureau or use a book (incriminating). Every decrypt used is a wager: exploit and risk the source, or hold.
- **Directive coverage:** Coding and deception primary. Secondary: items (code books, inks), economy (tariffs, bureau fees), politics (wartime censorship), travel (intercepts worth a train), hunt (reading hunters' traffic), time (censor delays).
- **Why captivating & addictive:** Puzzle "aha" and competence, a skill acquired, not a stat raised; novices progress while experts earn faster, cheaper intelligence; the source-burning dilemma is tension with legible consequence.
- **Engine implications:** String-level encryption of templated plaintexts with a crib vocabulary; the abstract rung returns identical outputs; messages as entities with routing paths, copy-holders and decay; per-office anomaly scoring with date-indexed parameters; per-faction hidden compromise-suspicion.
- **Historical grounding:** Bentley's code; Playfair in both wars; naval code books captured 1914; Room 40; ADFGVX 1918; Okhrana black cabinets; wartime postal censorship.
- **Provenance:** P05.05 (lead), P03.03, P03.02, P09.08; M-P05-3; draws on P05.04 (C13), P02.11 (C03).
- **Support:** 2 (P03, P05); merge proposed by P05 (P01, P02 concur via flags).
- **Flags addressed:** P09.08 (×2) merged — source-burning and the teaching staircase kept; the paid rung always exists; plaintexts templated per P02.
