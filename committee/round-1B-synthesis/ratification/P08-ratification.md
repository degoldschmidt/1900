## Amendments (≤3; only if AMEND)
### A-P08-1 — clarify — C13
- **Change:** In C13's Central hook replace "you see its estimated front" with "you see a self-forecast of its front, computed from your own visible acts and the published latency of each channel"; in How it plays replace "the notoriety map shows where your description has reached" with "the planner's self-forecast shows where your description has probably reached"; add to Engine implications: "the self-forecast reads only the player's own act log and channel parameters, never faction stores; what hunters actually hold surfaces only through C16's windows." Name this self-forecast as the same object as C02's "exposure forecast" and C17's "estimate of your trail".
- **Reason:** As drafted, C13 gives the player a direct readout of where hunter knowledge has reached — exactly the minimap that C16's central hook (support 3) is built to deny and that C13's own lead text (P01.06) lists as its first risk ("visualising a probabilistic front without creating a minimap exploit"). The two candidates currently contradict each other on the one question that decides whether forty hours of being hunted feel fair (Directive 4). A self-forecast from the player's own acts preserves C13's legible dread while keeping actual hunter belief behind C16's costly windows, and one definition makes C02, C13 and C17 consistent instead of three slightly different forecasts.

### A-P08-2 — merge — C05
- **Change:** Add P08.11 (Poacher and Gamekeeper) to C05's provenance as a member, and add to C05's How it plays: "Inverse mode: between fugitive stretches the player may take counter-espionage or inquiry commissions and run the same engine from the other chair — allocating watchers to termini, piers and frontier posts, filing liaison requests that politics may refuse, pruning hypotheses with the timetable, choosing when to spring a cordon (early and leaky, or late and expensive) — against an AI quarry that leaves records, buys decoys and dry-cleans; the hunter's own expense claims and cables are records too." Add to Engine implications: "hypotheses exposed as a weighted board for the human hunter; the quarry agent reuses the player-facing trace systems."
- **Reason:** A nominated proposal dropped although the Rapporteur's own note calls it "a cheap extension of C05" (both roles share one simulation, so content cost is near zero). It is the only proposal in the pool that makes the hunter AI legible by use, which is what C05's own retention claim ("the hunter is a mind, not a meter") depends on; the Scotland Yard touchstone is a two-seated game and the pool otherwise gives the player only the fugitive's chair. It also supplies the mechanics for an ending C11 already names — "turning hunter" — which at present has no system behind it anywhere in the twenty.

### A-P08-3 — clarify — C17
- **Change:** Add to C17's Engine implications: "C17 is the single record substrate of the hunt: C02's trace vectors, C05's delivered evidence, C03's faction stores, C08's publication events, C13's arriving facts, C14's witness log, C16's autopsy provenance and C19's cargo records are rows or filtered views of this one log; C13's 'facts scheduling arrival events at institution nodes' is this log's lag-and-reader model, not a second propagation mechanism; C16's autopsy replays C05's inference over C17's records." Add one sentence to the One-line pitch or Central hook stating that every other hunt candidate reads this store and none keeps its own.
- **Reason:** The consumers point at C17 but C17 never declares the dependency, and C13's engine note specifies its own fact-propagation layer. Two mechanisms for "what a service knows" would let hunter knowledge diverge between candidates — a fact that has "arrived" for the bourse but not "reached" the dossier — which breaks the invariant every hunt candidate's fairness argument rests on: that every deduction is derivable from one delivered-evidence log (P02.05's "one log drives save, replay and the hunt"). The Rapporteur's tally already records that raw support understates C17 as the pool's spine; this makes the substrate explicit in the candidate text, which is where a voter will read it.
