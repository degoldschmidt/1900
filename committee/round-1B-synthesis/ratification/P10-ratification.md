## Amendments (≤3; only if AMEND)
### A-P10-1 — clarify — C08
- **Change:** Restore P10.08 (a counted member of C08) to the text. In the Central hook, make the ledger three accounts, not two: typed instruments, dated debts, and an obligations ledger of dated lines owed to people rather than institutions — household staff, a burnt sub-agent's dependants, a patron owed favours, an informant on retainer, a liaison — each a tuple of demand frequency, cost, loyalty state and leverage, never a character. In How it plays, add: missed demands convert into leverage (letters, denunciations, saleable debts of honour); hunters and rival services pressure a dependant to produce an approach event the player may not detect; the verbs are discharge (expensive, final), hide (move or rename the line) or cut (loyalty turns hostile and the leverage activates). In Engine implications, add: obligation entities with calendars, loyalty decay and leverage payloads referencing legends; hunter target selection over the obligation graph.
- **Reason:** C08's provenance counts P10.08 and both C12 and C14 "draw on P10.08", yet C08's pitch contains no dependant, pensioner, patron, leverage or approach mechanic: the merge reduced "everyone you owe is a hunter" (M-P10-4, nominated by three panelists) to money alone. The Secretary's own lens question for this round names dependants; without this paragraph the life has no one in it to protect or abandon, and the moral weight the ledger was meant to generate without a script is lost.

### A-P10-2 — clarify — C02
- **Change:** Restore the three P10.07 rules that the merge compressed into "hardship accrues as health stocks (fatigue, disease, slow wounds)". In How it plays, add: (1) recovery runs in weeks of world time during which neither the world clock nor the hunt pauses, so convalescence is a forced wait that doubles as a planning phase (correspondence, cipher work, delegated business); (2) treatment writes records — a sanatorium season, a hospital admission, a prescription, a doctor's bill — as items and ledger entries in C17, so a sanatorium hides and registers you at once, and a limp or a scar is a portrait-parlé identifier; (3) age from C11's life clock lowers health ceilings and lengthens every timer, and chronic conditions (tuberculosis, syphilis, gas-damaged lungs from 1915, an old fracture) are permanent modifiers with flare rules. In the Day paragraph add that cities carry seasonal disease exposure, so routing is also health routing. In Engine implications, add treatment as scheduled multi-week actions coupled to the hunt and travel graphs.
- **Reason:** C02 declares "the life (health, ageing) primary" and counts P10.07 as a member, but keeps only the dial's health cost, not the body's own clock, its paper trail or ageing's effect; those are the mechanics that make the body a system interlocking with time pressure (directive 4) and the hunt rather than a fatigue bar, and ageing otherwise appears in no candidate as a rule, only as a word in C11's audit.

### A-P10-3 — clarify — C11
- **Change:** Move P10.05 from "draws on" to a counted member of C11 (support is unchanged, since P10 already counts for C11) and write its rules into How it plays and Engine implications as the content of the life clock: skills (languages by social register, cipher work, forgery, marksmanship, riding, motoring, wireless operation, bookkeeping) grow only by use, never by purchase; disuse decays effective level toward a retained floor, so mastery returns at a discount but costs time at the wrong moment; rusty skills emit tells, which are witness records for C17; age bends the curves (physical skills decline early, cognitive late, social grow longest); the period moves the skill map (motoring rare in 1900 and expected of a class by 1920; wireless operation a trade born inside the span; typewriting ordinary, with typefaces identifying machines). State that C20's "roll" rung and C12's bluff resolution read these values, so player-head mastery (hand-solving, route knowledge) and character skill coexist on one ladder.
- **Reason:** Directive 2 asks for a life sim / RPG; P10.05 is the pool's only character-progression model and the only thing that the ageing claimed by C02 and C11 can act upon. The Rapporteur attached it as an uncounted cross-reference and C11 retains only the words "skill rust", so the mechanic is mis-homed and watered down, while C20's abstraction ladder already presupposes a character skill value that no candidate defines.
