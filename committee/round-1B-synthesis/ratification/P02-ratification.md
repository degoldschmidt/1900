## Amendments (≤3; only if AMEND)
### A-P02-1 — correct — C11
- **Change:** In C11's Engine implications replace "year-boundary batched evaluation and compaction" with "year-boundary snapshots and batched evaluation; compaction applies only to derived state (standings, stocks, decay, valuations). The event record itself is append-only and is never rewritten or pruned, because C03's archives, C06's custody chains, C10's inquest and C16's autopsy reference events by id, and replay from snapshot plus input log must stay exact."
- **Reason:** As drafted, compacting the life record at year boundaries would dangle the event ids that C03 ("archive nodes store event ids, not copies"), C06 (provenance walked lazily on inspection), C10 ("every delta carries its causing event id") and C16 ("seed-replayable with provenance on every inference") depend on, and would break the one-record-store spine that the Rapporteur's breadth note credits C07, C17, C10 and C19 with carrying. The fix costs nothing: records are sparse (linear in player actions), only derived state needs compaction, and P10.11's own text already says "deterministic replay from audit snapshots", which is the snapshot model, not log rewriting.

### A-P02-2 — merge — C05
- **Change:** Add P08.11 (Poacher and Gamekeeper: The Hunter's Board) to C05's provenance as a mode, with this text in How it plays: "Between fugitive stretches the player may take counter-espionage or inquiry commissions and operate the same hypothesis board against an AI quarry: delayed ledger extracts arrive; watchers are allocated to termini, piers and frontier posts within a budget; foreign police are cabled and may refuse; hypotheses are pruned with Bradshaw's; the cordon is sprung early and leaky or late and expensive; hunting writes the player's own records (expense claims, cables)." And in Engine implications: "the hypothesis set is exposed as a weighted board usable by a human; the quarry agent reuses the player-facing trace systems; no second simulation."
- **Reason:** P08.11 was nominated and is one of only two nominated proposals with no home; the Rapporteur names it "a cheap extension of C05 and a natural amendment target". From the engine side it is nearly free (one simulation serves both roles) and it is the strongest proof of directive 4's fairness: if a human can run the hunt from the same delivered records, the AI hunter is legible by construction. It also produces the human-readable belief board that P04.06 and P08.05 both list as a tuning and debugging need, so it pays for its own development.

### A-P02-3 — correct — C10
- **Change:** Add to C10's Engine implications: "C10's tiered, dated schema is the single parameter layer over the two graphs: C06's edge document-requirement tables, C09's regime overlays, ratchets and military edge loads, C03's cooperation/extradition matrix, C13's censorship and cable-cut rewiring and C02's registration coverage are rows of one table keyed by (edge or jurisdiction, date, tier), written by macro-events and read by every other candidate; no candidate keeps a private regime table." Add the clause "reads the C10 parameter layer" to the Engine implications of C03, C06 and C09.
- **Reason:** As drafted, four candidates each describe their own dated regime layer (C06 "edge requirement tables by route, nationality, date"; C09 "regime overlays as modifier stacks on edges, prices and law"; C03 "cooperation/extradition matrix (service × jurisdiction × date)"; C10 "a regional tension field"). Authored separately they can disagree on the same date (a frontier closed by C09's overlay but open in C06's table; a liaison channel open in C03 that C09's war regime has severed), and the authoring surface multiplies, against directive 5's requirement of an efficient and scalable engine. One layer owned by the macro DAG, whose Tier 0 and Tier 1 events already write C09's rungs and ratchets, is what the Rapporteur's own breadth note ("two graphs with a regime layer") presupposes.
