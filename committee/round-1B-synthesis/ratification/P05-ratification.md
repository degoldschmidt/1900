## Amendments (≤3; only if AMEND)
### A-P05-1 — clarify — C17
- **Change:** In C17's "Central hook" and "Engine implications", name the canonical fact type and state that claims are records: every entry in the store is one tuple (subject, predicate, value, confidence, source, time, authorship, readers). World-emitted records (a hotel slip, a ticket, a cable copy, a frontier stamp) and identity-asserted claims (an Assert in C12, a report token in C01, a field on a forged paper in C06, an entry on a hotel form in C04) are the same type, differing only in the authorship field; a faction's dossier is its filtered view of both; a lie's truth-distance (C12) is the difference between a claim and the world log. Add one sentence to the engine lines of C01, C04, C06 and C12: "belief stores, legend fact-graphs, inspectors' fact stores and NPC belief sets are C17 views." Add to C01 and C04 the consequence this makes computable: a reconciliation at liaison between two services that know the player under different legends is itself a linkage-event source.
- **Reason:** Compatibility with the evidence engine (the Secretary's lens question). The five candidates currently describe five stores — C01 "a provenance-tagged fact store per identity", C04 "fact graphs audited by hunter queries", C06 "the inspector's fact store", C12 "bounded belief sets ... lies as propositions with holder set", C17 "a dossier is a filtered view" of records — with no stated relation. Without one schema the engine needs separate reconciliation (C01), verification (C04), inspection (C06), Call (C12) and deduction (C17) code paths over different types, against directive 5's "efficient and scalable engine". Nothing new is added: it is P02.10's tuple and C17's own hidden authorship field made canonical, as recorded in my 1B-1 role-lens audit (four representations, one schema).

### A-P05-2 — merge — C04
- **Change:** Add P10.04 (Circles of Repute) to C04's members (it remains "draws on" in C12) and write its rule into C04's "Central hook" and "How it plays": standing is held per name per circle (an officers' mess, a bourse, an émigré colony, a service's desk), decays toward neutral with time and distance, and gossip crosses bridging institutions (a bank, a club, a consulate) at a rate — so a scandal under one name surfaces elsewhere weeks later and, where one person sits in two circles, becomes a linkage event alongside handwriting, a trunk under two names and an endorsed draft; hunters read notoriety as ambient awareness. Engine line: sparse name × circle standing vectors; propagation along bridges with rate and decay, breadth-capped per tick.
- **Reason:** A nominated proposal (P05) carried only as a cross-reference, and a mechanic the merge lost: P10.04 is the only proposal that gives reputation and rumour a propagation mechanism on the social graph (P05.07's rumours are rightly bounded by the flag disposition, but that leaves no propagation at all). The draft reduces it to "the life (standing per circle)" in C12's coverage, dropping the gossip-bridge propagation and its name-linking consequence. Its natural home is C04, whose central mechanic is the linkage event and whose engine note ("identity-tagged evidence with a link-detection pass") otherwise obtains links only from physical evidence. Support counting is unchanged: P05 and P10 already support C04.

### A-P05-3 — merge — C05
- **Change:** Attach P08.11 (Poacher and Gamekeeper: The Hunter's Board) to C05 as an inverse-role mode, listed as "draws on" (support uncounted): between fugitive stretches the player may take counter-espionage or inquiry commissions and hunt an AI quarry on the same hypothesis-set engine — receiving delayed ledger extracts (C17), allocating watchers to termini, piers and frontier posts, cabling foreign police who may refuse (C03), pruning hypotheses with Bradshaw's and choosing when to spring the cordon early and leaky or late and expensive; the quarry runs on the inverted engine and lays decoys; the player's hunting writes records too (expense claims, cables). One sentence each in C05's "How it plays" and "Engine implications" (the hypothesis board must be human-usable). No new candidate; nothing displaced.
- **Reason:** Nominated but not carried, and named by the Rapporteur as a natural amendment target; by P08.11's own engine note both roles share one simulation, so the cost is small. It resolves C05's recorded risk — "conveying the model without exposing it" — by letting the player learn the hunter's reasoning by operating it, which in turn makes C17's false-evidence economy and C12's interrogation legible rather than asserted. Carried as a mode of C05 rather than a standalone candidate, it answers P01's and P10's objection (a slice, not a life) and pre-empts a swap-in that would displace a ranked candidate.
