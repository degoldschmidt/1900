## Amendments (≤3; only if AMEND)
### A-P09-1 — merge — C05
- **Change:** Add P08.11 (Poacher and Gamekeeper: The Hunter's Board) to C05's provenance as a member and write it in as a secondary mode. Append to "How it plays": "Between fugitive stretches, take a counter-espionage or inquiry commission and run the same engine from the other chair: receive delayed ledger extracts, post watchers at termini, piers and frontier posts, cable foreign police who may refuse, prune hypotheses with Bradshaw's, and spring the cordon early and leaky or late and expensive; the AI quarry leaves traces, buys decoys and dry-cleans; your own expense claims and cables write records about you." Append to "Why captivating & addictive": "Skill transfers across the chairs — a good hunter becomes a better fugitive, felt as mastery — and playing the hunter is the honest tutorial for the belief engine, which is otherwise learned only by being caught; the symmetry makes the AI's pursuit credible and legible." Append to "Engine implications": "no extra content cost — both roles share one simulation; the hypothesis set must be exposed as a human-readable weighted board." Title and support unchanged (P08 already supports C05).
- **Reason:** A nominated proposal wrongly dropped: the Rapporteur's own "not carried" note calls it "a cheap extension of C05 and a natural amendment target". From the retention lens it is the pool's strongest competence-transfer hook and the only mechanism that teaches the hypothesis engine (C05, C16) by doing rather than by failing — fairness the player has felt from the hunter's side is fairness they trust for forty hours as quarry. P08.11's own engine note shows the cost is near zero.
