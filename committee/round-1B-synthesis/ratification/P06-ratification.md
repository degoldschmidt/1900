## Amendments (≤3; only if AMEND)
### A-P06-1 — clarify — C08
- **Change:** In "How it plays", after "an advance recalled as coerced tasking", add: "Debts are owed by a name, but ruin is a disclosure, not an escape: protest and gazette notices are records in C17's ledger; a creditor of a burned legend becomes a private hunter (C03's inquiry agents) holding that legend's dossier, with a budget proportional to the debt, seeking the linkage (C04) that attaches it to your other names; lenders size credit to a legend's record-set depth, so a thin name cannot borrow enough to make burning it pay. The same rule binds C15's firm: bankruptcy passes the books to a receiver, whose examination is public." In "Engine implications", add: "creditor agents escalate into hunter entities that read C17 and C03."
- **Reason:** C08's "Flags addressed" states that debt-shedding was "bounded per P06's audit", but no bounding mechanism survives in the candidate text, while the hook keeps "identity-scoped liabilities" and "insolvency a playable reset". Without the rule, borrow-under-A-then-burn-A is the dominant strategy (P06.08's own risk note; P06 audit). This restores a mechanic the merge lost, using only members and cross-references the draft already cites (P06.08, P10.09; P08.04 in C03; P08.03 in C04). Historically grounded: Bankruptcy Act 1883 public examination; official receivers under the Companies (Winding Up) Act 1890.

### A-P06-2 — clarify — C18
- **Change:** In "How it plays", after "match window to want before either closes", add: "Factions pay on corroboration: a delivery is scored against the faction's other sources after a verification lag (hours at a bourse, weeks at a registry); a fabricated window is paid provisionally and, when contradicted, reverses as credit loss and suspicion (C01's consistency debt); a secret sold twice loses its exclusivity premium and the copies carry marks (C06)." In "Engine implications", add: "payment events carry a verification timer and a reversal branch."
- **Reason:** The P05.10 disposition homed "services pay on corroboration" in C06, an item candidate, but payment is buyer behaviour and C18 is where the buyers live. C18 currently inherits P01.02's "a want without a window can be met by forgery" with no payment condition, so the money printer P06 flagged in P05.10 reappears here. C01 already states the rule for intelligence services; C18 must state it for every faction buyer (ministries, firms, newspapers) or the demand engine is exploitable. Pay-by-results was period practice for freelance agents.

### A-P06-3 — clarify — C06
- **Change:** In "Engine implications", replace "provenance as an event log walked lazily on inspection against the inspector's fact store" with "provenance is C17's record store filtered by item id (issued, bought, stolen, copied, forged, crossed a frontier at a time, pawned, planted); the inspector's known facts are its institution's C17 view as widened by C03 liaison; C19's luggage and cargo write to the same store." In "Central hook", after "inspectors have depth profiles", add: "an examination is a probabilistic depth draw (inspector profile, the bearer's plausibility, class, bribes, regime) followed by a deterministic field-by-field resolution against the item and the inspector's facts."
- **Reason:** C06 ("every frontier is a dice-free test"), C17 ("one append-only record store") and C19 ("inspected with odds shaped by plausibility and bribes"; "cargo writes records into C17's ledger") describe compatible models that the draft never links, and the dice-free/odds wording reads as a contradiction between two candidates. The one-log rule is P02's engine spine and P06's audit ("adopt that schema once"); the two-step examination reconciles P05.06's deterministic inspection with P04.03's examination probability, both members of these candidates. Nothing is added that a member proposal did not already specify.
