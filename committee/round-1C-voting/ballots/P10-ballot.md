## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
C04 is the one concept every other candidate already presupposes: a legend is the unit of account. Money (C08) is a ledger per name; traces (C17) are records under a name; hunters (C05, C03) keep a dossier per name and advance only by linking two; standing, households, debts and nationality attach to names, not to the player. That is the touchstone career made mechanical, several identities and households in several cities with a past that catches up, and it realises all five directives at once: lives run in real cities under real registration regimes (Britain invents, Germany audits), travel is the circuit that keeps lives provably incompatible, deception is what each name asserts, the hunt is linkage, and the world clock (1914 internment, 1915 photographs) raises the price of every name you kept. Its hook is honest: endowment over lives you built, and granular failure, lose a name, not the game. Nine panelists proposed it independently.
