## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
C01 is the touchstone career as a system: several services and principals pay you at once, each holding a belief store about you, and your product is the report, composed from held tokens and fabrications and scored for novelty and corroboration. Every directive hangs from that loop. Politics is the patron and the calendar of reckonings (1907, 1914, 1917) at which stores are compared; deception is the report and the consistency debt paid by making lies true; economy is retainers and a budget loop in which your credited successes fund the hunters who will read you; travel is handlers in several capitals; the hunt is the patron who flips; time pressure is a liaison date you can see coming. It needs C10 as its world and C17 as its record, and arranges both. Its hook is honest — dramatic irony, near-misses you controlled, betrayal born of your own inconsistencies — and seven of ten panelists converged on it independently.
