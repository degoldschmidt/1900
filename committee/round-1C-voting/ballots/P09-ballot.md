## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
Build the timetable first. C07 makes the world a discrete-event simulation whose only decision points are departures: every verb (a meeting, a cable, a forgery, a bank drawing) has a duration and competes for the slack before the 14:05, so travel, deception, items, economy and politics are one game before any of them is deep. The hunt reads the same Bradshaw, the diary is the scheduler's face, and the engine is the fifth directive made playable: one priority queue, seed plus input log, exact replay. Its hook is the pool's most honest: Zeigarnik tension from a booked departure, the near-miss of a connection made by eleven minutes, slack as a mastery curve, and sessions that end aboard a night train, the natural stopping point players refuse. Routes as owned, dated knowledge give thirty years of progression that lives in the player's head. Everything else attaches to this skeleton.
