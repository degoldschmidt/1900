## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
C07 is the spine because it is the one scarce resource every other system spends: time on a printed schedule. Travel (directive 3) is its skeleton; time pressure (directive 4) is native, since departures leave without you and the hunters read the same Bradshaw; deception, items, economy and politics are actions with durations competing for the slack between connections; the life is a diary of rent days. It is historically exact as mechanics: the monthly Guide, the transfer at the gauge break, bank hours, ice closures, a network that changes by date. Its compulsion is the proven, honest kind: the near-missed connection, the booked departure not yet earned, sessions that end aboard a night train. For directive 5 it is literally the engine: a global priority queue with total ordering, so a save is seed plus input log and every consequence is a scheduled event. All nineteen other candidates attach to it; none runs without it.
