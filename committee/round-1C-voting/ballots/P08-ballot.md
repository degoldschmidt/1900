## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
The Legend Portfolio is the spine because identity is the join key of this entire game. Every other candidate is already written per name: C08's ledger, C17's records, C05's dossiers, C06's papers, C07's circuits, standing per circle. Build the legend as the unit of account and the other nineteen attach without seams. It is directive 2 verbatim — several identities, households in several cities, the past catching up — and it is Scotland Yard transposed from location to identity: hunters never see you, only names, and advance only by linking two, with the timetable-incompatibility test making that link decidable and auditable. Failure is granular — lose a name, not the game — which is what lets forty hours of pursuit stay fair. The history is mechanical, not decorative: Britain is where names are invented until 1915, Germany where they die. Engine cost is bounded: unworn identities run at low detail.
