## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
The period's most pervasive constraint on a spy was the schedule: trains, steamers, mails, bank hours and registration deadlines ran on printed timetables, and C07 makes that schedule the game's turn structure. Every verb (a meeting, a cable, a forgery, a drawing at a bank) spends time in the slack between departures, so travel (directive 3), time pressure (directive 4) and the engine's single scheduler (directive 5) are one object, and the spy's life (directive 2) becomes a diary read against Bradshaw's. Accuracy is mechanics, not decoration: monthly editions go stale, the Russian gauge break forces a change of train, 1914 deletes services, tunnels and airlines shrink the world across thirty years. Every other candidate plugs into its slots: hunters on the same graph, reconciliations on alliance dates, quarter-days, mobilisation overwriting the civilian timetable. Its pull is proven and honest: one more leg, sessions ending aboard a night train.
