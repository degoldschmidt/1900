## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
C15 is the only candidate whose hook is the touchstone's own career: commerce and espionage as one loop, the private life paid from the same books. It answers directive 2 literally — spy career, business career and personal life as one resource system, hunted through its own records — and gives every other system its reason. Travel, because grants sit in Constantinople, Tehran, Peking. Deception, because the firm is a legend with paper. Items, because deeds, tenders and ledgers are artifacts. Politics, because grants are won through legations and voided in 1908, 1914, 1917. Time pressure, because decrees, options and bills mature. The hunt, because the books hang you and bankruptcy hands them to a receiver. Its compulsion is honest tycoon psychology — long payoffs, near-solvency, sell or hold — under a life-and-death hunt on sunk costs. Project objects with political preconditions are a direct, feasible test of the engine.
