## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
Build the clock first. C07 makes the timetable the turn: every verb in the game — meet, cable, forge, trade, bribe, register — is an action with a duration placed in the slack between departures, so travel, deception, items, economy and politics all spend the one resource the hunters also read. That is what turns six deep systems into one game rather than six minigames. It is directive 5 made literal: one priority queue of timestamped events is the engine, the save (seed plus input log) and the world that moves without you. It realises Bradshaw's and 80 Days as mechanics — routes as owned, dated knowledge, the connection as the atomic unit of tension — and its compulsion is the honest kind: one more leg, generalised to a thirty-year life. Everything else in the twenty (C17's records, C02's prices, C01's masters, C18's jobs) hangs from this spine without modification.
