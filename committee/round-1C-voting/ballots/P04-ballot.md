## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
C07 is the one candidate that is a frame rather than a feature. It makes the world a timetable: every verb the other nineteen propose, a forged paper, a bribe, a bill falling due, takes hours and must fit between departures that leave whether or not you are ready. That satisfies Directive 3's Bradshaw's and 80 Days touchstones literally (real connections, fares, classes, owned and stale route knowledge on a date-versioned, multi-modal network), Directive 4 structurally (the world advances through the same queue; hunters ride and read the same timetable) and Directive 5 cheaply (one priority queue, exact replay from seed plus input log, connection-scan routing). Its addiction is honest: the connection just made, the booked departure not yet earned, the night train that ends a session mid-journey. Arrange C13's second graph, C05's hunters, C04's legends and C18's jobs around it and you have one game, not six.
