## Champion statement (≤150 words) for your #1
C01 is the touchstone career made mechanical: several paymasters each hold a model of you, and every lie you tell is a debt that a dated reconciliation can call in. As a spine it arranges the other systems without absorbing them: C12 is how claims are made, C17 is what records exist, C04 is which name they attach to, C20 is the cipher each service reads, C03 and C10 are the alliance calendar that schedules the reckonings, C05 and C16 are what happens when a belief flips to double. Its hook is honest: dramatic irony, near-misses you controlled, each added master a self-set difficulty slider; and its engine is bounded by design, since stores are compared only at liaison. It invents nothing: 1904, 1907, 1914 and 1917 are the reckonings, and the budget loop means your successes fund the hunters who will one day read you.
