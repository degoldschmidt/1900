# Hook-honesty audit — C01 Several Masters, One Truth
## Verdict
Honest and specific — dread and relief are caused by the player's own lies on a calendar that is history — but the argument omits its best mechanism, the budget loop, and must say audit cadences are learnable.
## Corrections
1. **Why it is captivating** — "dread of the cross-check at each audit and liaison date" → say audit cadences are learnable (the example's quarterly British audit) and liaison dates are Tier 0, so tension climbs toward known dates with unknown content → scheduled uncertainty motivates; diffuse dread churns.
2. **Why it is captivating** — the budget loop is absent → add: every credited success funds the hunters who will read you, so difficulty rises by the player's own achievement, never by rubber-banding → persisting consequence is the rarest honest hook, and this design has it by construction.
3. **Why it is captivating** — "relief at each paid, corroborated report" → add that payment follows a verification lag, so relief is a delayed, legible event rather than a click → delayed payoff is the stronger anticipation loop, honest because the lag is institutional.
4. **Why it is captivating** — "P09's standard is met" → drop the attribution, keep the mechanism (the leak that nearly crossed a threshold came from a lie the player chose) → the argument should stand without the committee.
5. **Progression and endings** — "a store flips to 'double', pay stops and the hunt begins" → confirm the ramp: rising suspicion is readable through C16's windows before the flip → a cliff without a ramp reads as arbitrary; the windows already exist in Risks.
## Session structure
Stopping points: a paid delivery (relief), the night before an audit (dread), a reckoning passed, the year-end "one more report, or stop". Players push through because the next requirement expires with its crisis, tomorrow's maturing lie is theirs to service, and the liaison date is in the newspapers. They will not when no lie is outstanding and no requirement is live; the generator must never leave a capital quiet.
## Notes
"Selling twice pays twice" is push-your-luck, honest only because copy marks are known before the sale; keep that visible. The self-set difficulty slider is the right frame; it must be reversible (dormant relationships cost nothing), which Risks confirms. No dark patterns found.
