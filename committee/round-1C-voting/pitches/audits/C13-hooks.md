## Session structure
Stopping points: the last edition, the bourse's close, the dark sea leg. Players push through because the session ends aboard a train, the forecast showing where the description will be on arrival, and the window closes at a published hour. They will not if the wire always wins or offices never matter; the MVP's tuning target is also the retention target.
