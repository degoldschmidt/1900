# Hook-honesty audit — C13 Faster Than the Wire
## Verdict
Honest, explicit (it disavows variable-ratio schedules outright) and mechanism-backed, but a checkpoint "the forecast rates at 40 per cent" is a dice roll unless the argument says the number decomposes, and the market's fast numeric reward is a retention risk treated only as balance.
## Corrections
1. **Core loop / Why it is captivating** — "a checkpoint the forecast rates at 40 per cent" → say the probability decomposes into channels, offices and liaison gates the player can act on (alight earlier; sign the other legend), as the worked example shows → a legible, changeable probability is agency; a bare percentage is a lottery with a label.
2. **Why it is captivating** — "the market makes the gap pay in numbers" → add that market windows exist only for facts whose acquisition is on record, so they are scarce by construction → an immediate numeric reward loop outcompetes the slow spy loops unless rare; emergent bounds limit profit, not frequency.
3. **Why it is captivating** — the forecast is presented only as dread management → add that it delivers relief honestly: it usually reports that no reader holds you yet, so standing still is a decision, not a gamble → relief earned by reading closes the tension cycle.
4. **Why it is captivating** — "Emergent narrative is the record itself, reconstructed in the autopsy" → sharpen: the autopsy shows the race's margin (the hour the description arrived), so near-misses are replayable → a reconstructed margin converts a loss into mastery.
5. **Why it is captivating** — "P09's ballot" quoted → drop the attribution, keep the mechanism → the argument should stand without the committee.
## Session structure
Stopping points: the last edition, the bourse's close, and the dark sea leg. Players push through because the session ends aboard a train with the forecast showing where the description will be at arrival, and the window closes at a published hour. They will not if the wire always wins (an unwinnable race) or offices never matter (a trivial one); the MVP's tuning target is also the retention target.
## Notes
The two-front structure (the secret's news and your own description) is the cleanest tension/relief design among the finalists: both fronts have published arrival times. Keep headlines structured; an unanswered headline is the information gap that sells the next edition, honest because the detail is purchasable.
