# Hook-honesty audit — C07 The Departure Is the Turn
## Verdict
Specific, honest and mechanism-backed — the strongest short-cycle argument of the five — but it opens by quoting the panel instead of arguing, and undersells two loops its own mechanics create.
## Corrections
1. **Why it is captivating** — four panelist quotations open the section → cut them; open with the mechanism (a booked departure is an open loop until its errands are done) → the owner needs the argument, not the committee's applause.
2. **Why it is captivating** — "80 Days' strongest reinforcement, and here every verb produces one" → "near-misses motivate only when the margin was the player's own slack; delay odds are shown before commitment, so every miss is attributable" → "reinforcement" is operant conditioning; an unforecast miss is frustration, not a hook.
3. **Why it is captivating** — mastery as growth only → add the loss half: editions go stale on a dated cadence (1 May, 1 October, wartime), so route knowledge rots on the period's own clock → an honest, scheduled reason to re-engage, already present in Progression.
4. **Why it is captivating** — the time jump is absent → name its anticipation: the diary shows what will pass; a sealed leg's arrival dumps the news as a reveal → information gap plus anticipation, both already in Risks.
5. **Risks** — "the slack must always have claimants" has no mechanism → name C18's refresh and C08's maturities as guaranteed claimants per city-day → an empty slot is dead time, the one state where this design loses the player.
## Session structure
Stopping points: the hotel register (costed), aboard the night train (the designed end), arrival after a sealed leg (a reveal), the year-end audit. Players push through because the departure board is visible, the booked departure is an unfinished task, and the 23:40 reaches Berlin before the bourse opens. They will not when a day has no claimant for its slack, or when off-screen resolution punishes without the promised warnings.
## Notes
No dark patterns: no real-time appointments, no variable-ratio schedule; the pause has a price, not a penalty. Say why players continue past the audit: the coming year's known closures stamped onto the calendar are anticipation, not obligation. Keep "one more leg" tied to visible departures, never to a timer.
