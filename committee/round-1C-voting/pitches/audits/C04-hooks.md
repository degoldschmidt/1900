# Hook-honesty audit — C04 The Legend Portfolio
## Verdict
Honest and mechanism-backed — endowment on built names and granular failure are correctly named — but two phrasings drift toward upkeep-nagging, and the argument ends on quotations instead of its own strongest point.
## Corrections
1. **Why it is captivating** — "the appointment compulsion of 'I must be in Petersburg before quarter-day'" → "deadline planning on the period's calendar" → "appointment" names a real-time dark pattern; here the quarter-day is an in-game constraint, not a summons.
2. **Why it is captivating** — "an unattended life is a day from going stale" → state the decay scale (quarter-days, weeks of unanswered mail), its legibility (the ledger) and its delegability (staff who leak) → invisible continuous decay is the withering-crop pattern; slow, legible, delegable decay is a planning constraint.
3. **Why it is captivating** — closes with two panelist quotations → replace with the mechanism: failure is granular, so a disaster ends a name and a new legend's fresh start keeps the career running → the owner needs the claim, not the attribution.
4. **Why it is captivating** — the linkage-risk indicator is absent → add: risk is previewed before commitment ("arriving as A on B's ticket"), so every link is attributable and every escape earned → visible odds before the act make dread agency, not anxiety.
5. **Core loop (session)** — "callers, fines, a creditor's letter, an offer at the club" → commit to a ratio: every arrival materialises at least one opportunity, not only obligations → arrivals are this design's reveals; if they deliver only chores, players stop there.
## Session structure
Stopping points: arrivals (a city's mail materialises), the end of a circuit, the burning of a legend. Players push through because a departure is already booked, a verification clock is running on yesterday's claim, and a new name offers a clean slate. They will not if arrivals deliver fines without offers, or if three to five lives become bookkeeping without the ledger Risks promises.
## Notes
Losing a legend is the honest form of permadeath: it hurts, it is attributable in the autopsy, and the career continues. Keep burning the player's choice; a legend linked by a hunter should read as the player's own record, never as bad luck. No variable-ratio or real-time mechanics found.
