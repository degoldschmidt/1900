# Historian's audit — C17 The Paper Hunter: One Evidence Engine

## Verdict
Accurate; three precision corrections (alien-registration photographs, letters under perlustration, the naval reader).

## Corrections
1. **A worked example of play** — "B presents papers at a police station, from February 1915 with a photograph" → the February 1915 photograph rule applied to British passports; aliens' police registration certificates acquired photographs under the wartime Aliens Restriction Orders (consolidated in 1916) → two dates, two documents.
2. **Core loop** — "a letter leaves nothing and takes three days" → true in pre-war Western Europe; in Russia the black cabinets perlustrated watch-listed mail before 1914, and from August 1914 every belligerent's censorship opened, stamped and logged letters → a letter's "no record" is a row of the dated layer, not a constant.
3. **A worked example of play** — "with the Hamburg police within a day; Abteilung IIIb on request" → a shipyard tender is naval intelligence; its German reader would be the Admiralty Staff's intelligence section (N) working with the Hamburg political police, Abteilung IIIb being the General Staff's section → readers follow the document's subject.
4. **Systems: Travel** — "in Britain only steerage appears on the Aliens Act 1905 lists" → correct, and narrower still: lists were required only of "immigrant ships" carrying more than twenty alien steerage passengers, so a Harwich packet filed none → the Hook route writes nothing at either end before 1914.

## Notes
The parameter table is the period: Meldezettel within a day, warrant-bound cable copies, the 1886 treaty beside the 1870 exception, Julian registers, Petersburg time, Ottoman clocks from sunset. Affordances the store could add cheaply: poste restante collection required proof of identity (a passport or visiting card) in most countries, so receiving a letter wrote a record at the counter; registered letters were signed for; and German hotels kept a Fremdenbuch open to inspection beside the slip they delivered, two records with different readers and lags.
